Affected Groups: People who have been subjected to pushback operations, informal deportations, and border removals conducted by non-state or proxy forces operating in border zones in Europe, the Middle East, and Africa, where EU member states or other countries have outsourced border enforcement to neighboring state militaries, militias, or coast guards, and who have been removed across borders without any legal process, any documentation, any named authority, and any mechanism through which the removal can be formally attributed to an accountable party.
Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical 
Mechanisms: Pushbacks violate the non-refoulement principle under the 1951 Refugee Convention and EU law, which prohibits the return of a person to a place where they face serious harm without an individual assessment. When the removal is carried out by forces that are not formally identified, the accountability gap is structural: no named body is responsible, no record of the removal exists. The correct legal terms include pushback, collective expulsion, and refoulement.
