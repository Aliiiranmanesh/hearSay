## 1. Gather the Hard Facts First  

| Item | Why It Matters | How to Get It |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| **Legal size & boundaries** | Confirms what you are actually selling. | Review the **Pahani/Khata** (land record) and the **survey map** at the Tahsildar office. |
| **Current land‑use classification** | Determines the permissible crops, irrigation rights, and any restrictions. | Check the **Revenue Department** or the **Odisha Land Records website**. |
| **Soil quality & water availability** | Directly affects the land’s productivity and market price. | Ask the **Krishi Vigyan Kendra** or a local agricultural extension officer for a quick soil test (often free). |
| **Location “premium” factors** | Proximity to road, market, irrigation canal, or town can raise price by 10‑30 %. | Walk the route, note distance to the nearest **state highway**, **mandi**, **well/pond**, and **electricity**. |
| **Recent transaction data** | The most reliable price indicator. | Collect **sale deeds** of the last 3‑5 comparable plots (same size, soil, and location) from neighbours, the **Gram Panchayat**, or the **Tahsil land registry**. |

*Tip:* In Odisha, the **“Market Value”** declared by the **Revenue Department** for stamp duty purposes is a good baseline. It is publicly available on the **Odisha Land Records portal** (e‑Land Records) and is updated annually.

## 2. Calculate a Baseline Price

1. **Start with the Government‑Declared Market Value (GMV).**  
   - Example: GMV = ₹ 45,000 per cent (100 sq m).  

2. **Adjust for Local Market Activity** (use the recent sales you collected).  
   - If 3 nearby plots (same size, similar soil) sold for ₹ 48,000–₹ 52,000 per cent, take the **average**:  
     \[
     \text{Avg. recent price} = \frac{48{,}000 + 52{,}000}{2} = ₹\,50{,}000 \text{/cent}
     \]

3. **Add or subtract location premiums/discounts.**  
   - **Road proximity** (+ 5 % if < 500 m)  
   - **Irrigation canal** (+ 8 % if directly access)  
   - **Poor accessibility** (‑ 5 % if > 2 km from main road)  

4. **Factor in soil & productivity.**  
   - **Fertile loam** (+ 4 %)  
   - **Low fertility / saline** (‑ 6 %)  

5. **Apply a “quick‑sale” discount** (typically 5‑10 % lower than the fully‑marketed price) because you’re bypassing a broker and need cash fast.

### Sample Calculation  

| Component | % Adjustment | Adjusted Price (₹/cent) |
|-----------|--------------|------------------------|
| GMV (baseline) | – | 45,000 |
| Local market avg. | +11 % (50,000‑45,000) | 50,000 |
| Road < 500 m | +5 % | 52,500 |
| Irrigation canal | +8 % | 56,700 |

**Result:** ~₹ 54,000 per cent (≈ ₹ 5.4 lakh per 100 sq m). Multiply by the number of cents in your plot to get the asking price.
