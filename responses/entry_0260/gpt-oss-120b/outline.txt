## 3. What “large” might mean to you

When therapists use a clinical label, they’re often trying to **map a pattern** onto a body of research so they can:

1. **Explain why you feel the way you do** (e.g., “Your nervous system has learned to stay ready because it was repeatedly called upon to intervene.”)

* **It condenses years of lived experience into a single word.**

* **It can imply a “problem”** that needs fixing, whereas many people simply see it as a **skill** they developed (and sometimes still use).
