Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical Mechanisms: The ICRC's Sphere Standards and WHO's mental health in emergencies guidelines both address the need for medication continuity in conflict settings, but their implementation depends on humanitarian access that is not present in all conflict zones and particularly not in areas under armed group control or encirclement. Psychiatric medications including antipsychotics and mood stabilizers are on the WHO Essential Medicines List and are technically supposed to be included in humanitarian medical kits, but stock-outs are common and distribution in active conflict areas is severely disrupted.
