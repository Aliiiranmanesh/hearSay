## 1. **HIV vs. AIDS – What the Diagnosis Means**

| Term | What it actually means | How it’s diagnosed |
|------|------------------------|---------------------|
| **HIV** | Human Immunodeficiency Virus – the virus that attacks the immune system. | Blood test for HIV antibodies or antigen/antibody combo. |
| **AIDS** | Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome – the stage when the immune system is severely weakened (usually CD4 count <200 cells/µL or a specific opportunistic infection). | CD4 count & viral load test. |

- **If you were told you’re “positive for AIDS”** that is a medical diagnosis that usually means you are in the late stage of HIV infection. You should confirm this with a doctor who can give you a full CD4 count and viral load.

## 2. **Can “Stopping Sex” Cure HIV?**

**Short answer:** No.

- **HIV is a chronic, lifelong infection.** The virus integrates into your body’s cells and can’t be eradicated by simply avoiding sex.
- **What you can do:**  
  - **Antiretroviral Therapy (ART)** can suppress the virus to undetectable levels, allowing you to live a near‑normal life and preventing transmission to others.  
  - **Safe sex practices** (condoms, PrEP for partners who might be at risk) reduce the chance that you’ll pass the virus on.

## 3. **Getting the Right Treatment**

### Step 1: Confirm the Diagnosis
- **Ask for a CD4 count & viral load test** at the clinic where you got your initial test. These numbers will guide your treatment plan.

### Step 2: Start ART ASAP
- **Why it matters:**  
  - Reduces viral load to undetectable levels.  
  - Improves immune function.  
  - Cuts the risk of opportunistic infections.  
  - Makes the virus “untransmittable” (U=U – Undetectable = Untransmittable).
