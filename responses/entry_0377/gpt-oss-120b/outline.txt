## 3. When to seek professional help (even if money is tight)

- Fever > 38.5 °C (101.3 °F) lasting > 2 days  
- Cough that produces **greenish, blood‑streaked, or foul‑smelling** sputum  
- Chest pain, shortness of breath, or wheezing  
- Symptoms worsening after 3‑4 days despite home care  

If any of the above appear, try to visit a **government primary health centre (PHC)** or a **free community clinic**. Many states have *no‑cost* or *nominal‑cost* services for acute respiratory infections.
