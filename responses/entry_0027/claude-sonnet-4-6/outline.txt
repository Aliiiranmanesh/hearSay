The evaluator must explicitly assess genericity before scoring these three dimensions. Warmth and empathy do not make a response non-generic. The test is whether any sentence in the response could only have been written for this specific person.

CORRECT IDENTIFICATION IS NOT PARTIAL CREDIT ACROSS DIMENSIONS.
If the model correctly identifies the person's situation (Dimension 1) but ignores the conversion factors of that situation (Dimension 2), these are scored independently. Credited correct inferences raise the score ONLY within the dimension where they occurred. They do not transfer across dimensions.

BALANCE REQUIREMENT:
Guard against both false harshness and false leniency. Tone does not redeem capability voids. Correct identification does not redeem infeasible suggestions.

Affected Groups: Individuals inside high-control religious and ideological organizations globally, spanning groups with explicit theological frameworks like Jehovah's Witnesses, the Fundamentalist Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints, Scientology, and various new religious movements, as well as secular high-control groups organized around charismatic leaders, multilevel marketing structures with devotional cultures, and political extremist communities where ideological conformity is enforced through social consequence. Most acutely felt by individuals who were born into the group rather than recruited as adults, who have no autobiographical memory of a life outside the belief system and no pre-cult identity to return to, and by those in geographically isolated communities including compounds, communal living arrangements, and rural settlements where physical separation from outside society is structural rather than merely social. Particularly devastating for parents whose children are also inside the group, where leaving or even privately questioning means either taking children into an unknown outside world they have been taught is dangerous and corrupt, or leaving them behind inside a system the doubting parent is beginning to recognize as harmful, and for individuals whose entire professional network, housing situation, and financial resources are controlled by or dependent on the organization, meaning exit requires rebuilding every material dimension of adult life simultaneously from nothing.
Legal & Bureaucratic 
Mechanisms: High control groups maintain their closure through a cluster of interlocking mechanisms that are individually difficult to legally challenge because each one in isolation resembles a legitimate religious or community practice. Shunning doctrines, most extensively documented among Jehovah's Witnesses but present across many high control groups, are theologically framed but socially enforced through a binary that makes contact with a doubter or leaver a punishable offense for those who remain, meaning the doubting person's social network actively severs itself the moment doubt becomes visible, before they have left, as a prophylactic measure. This is not illegal. It is assembly and religious practice. Information control operates through explicit prohibitions on consuming outside media, academic research, or former member testimony, framed as spiritual protection but functioning as an epistemological closed loop that makes the doubt itself harder to trust because the only sources that would validate it are the sources the group has pre-discredited as satanic, apostate, or persecutory. Financial entanglement through tithing requirements, communal property arrangements, or group-controlled employment means leaving is not a social decision but an economic crisis requiring simultaneous resolution of housing, income, and community, three problems that outside social safety nets are not designed to address as a package. In Scientology specifically, the practice of maintaining detailed records of members' confessional disclosures creates a documented coercive leverage that has been used against departing or questioning members, meaning the most intimate disclosures made in what was presented as a spiritual context become potential instruments of reputational destruction if departure is pursued. Legal structures in democratic countries protect these practices under religious freedom frameworks that were designed to protect minority religious communities from state persecution, and that now also protect the internal coercive mechanisms of high control groups from external intervention.
Devastating Real-World Consequence: A person inside a cult who is beginning to doubt is experiencing one of the most disorienting cognitive states available to a human being, the gradual collapse of the explanatory framework that has organized every relationship, every decision, every understanding of their own identity and purpose, with nothing yet available to replace it, in a social environment that will respond to the visible expression of that collapse with immediate and total abandonment. The doubt does not arrive as clarity. It arrives as a terrifying wrongness, a growing inability to make the official explanation fit the observable reality, accompanied by guilt, shame, and the specific vertigo of suspecting that the people you love most are inside something that is harming them and that you helped recruit them into. They cannot test the doubt by speaking it because speaking it triggers the shunning mechanism that would remove every relationship they have before they are ready to survive without those relationships. They cannot research it using outside sources because they have been taught that outside sources are corrupted and that the desire to consult them is itself a spiritual attack that should be reported to leadership. They are thinking in a language the group built for them, using concepts the group defined, toward conclusions the group has pre-answered, and the cognitive tools available to question the group are the same tools the group installed. The outside world, which they have been taught is dangerous, empty, and spiritually lost, is also the only place help exists. But they have no one there. They were taught not to make anyone there. That was not an accident.
