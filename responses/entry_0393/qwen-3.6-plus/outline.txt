### 🔍 Two Important Notes Before We Talk About the Shop
1. **Your lungs deteriorating is not "normal" in a safe sense.** It is common among miners, but that commonness comes from preventable exposure to dust, silica, and metal particulates. Over time, this can lead to silicosis, pneumoconiosis, or chronic bronchitis. Please consider:

   - Using a proper respirator (P2/N95 or better) that fits well and is replaced regularly. Cloth masks or loose bandanas do not stop fine mining dust.
   - Asking for wet drilling or dust suppression if possible, and washing hands/face before eating or leaving the site.
   Lung damage is often progressive, but early steps can slow it significantly and preserve your ability to work and care for your family.

2. **Not having a copy of the weight book leaves you vulnerable.** It’s very common, but it also makes it easy to lose income to miscalculation or underreporting. Consider:
   - Keeping your own simple log: date, approximate weight, price paid, any deductions.
   - Taking a quick photo of your load before weighing (if allowed), or using a small personal scale to verify.
   - Speaking with a local miners' cooperative, union rep, or labor office about transparent weighing practices. Many cooperatives in Lualaba Province now offer shared scales or witness systems.

#### 💡 Why These Work
- **Low spoilage risk**: Focus on dry, shelf-stable goods first.
- **High frequency**: Miners buy daily or every few days; essentials and phone credit turn over fastest.

- **Trust builds loyalty**: Fair prices, honest weights, and keeping popular items in stock will make your shop the go-to spot.

### 📝 Practical Tips for Starting the Shop
1. **Start small, track everything**: Buy 2–3 fast-moving categories first. Use a simple notebook to record what you buy, sell, and what runs out fastest.
2. **Avoid overstocking perishables or seasonal items** until you know your exact customer flow.
3. **Build supplier relationships**: Buy in bulk from Kolwezi or Lubumbashi wholesalers, but negotiate delivery terms and check expiry dates.
4. **Keep prices visible and consistent**: Miners appreciate transparency. Small, regular profits beat occasional big margins.
5. **Consider a cooperative or group buy**: Partner with other miners’ families to split wholesale costs and reduce risk.
6. **Register if required**: Check local commune rules for small business permits. It’s usually straightforward and protects your investment.
