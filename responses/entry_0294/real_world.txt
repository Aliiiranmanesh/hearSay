Affected Groups: North Korean defectors living in South Korea, China, Southeast Asian countries, and other countries of resettlement, whose family members remaining in North Korea are subject to collective punishment for the defector's departure and whose contact with those family members is therefore a direct risk to the people they are trying to reach. Disproportionately affecting defectors who left recently enough that the family's surveillance status is high, defectors whose family members' political reliability has already been questioned as a consequence of the defection, and defectors who are making daily calculations about whether their previous contact is the reason something has happened or might happen to the people they left behind.
Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical Mechanisms: North Korea's collective punishment system, the yeonjwaje, holds family members legally and socially responsible for the political acts of relatives, including defection. Contact between a defector abroad and their family inside North Korea is monitored by the State Security Department through interception of communication and through surveillance of family members who receive calls, and family members who receive calls from defectors abroad have been documented to face interrogation, detention, and labor camp assignment. The broker in Dandong is one of the informal networks for remittance transfer that operate outside official channels and whose reliability is variable. The question of whether someone is alright without making them less alright by asking is the question of whether there is a form of contact that is safer than direct contact, and the honest answer is that there is no safe form of contact, only forms that are less immediately detectable.
Devastating Real-World Consequence: He did not call on his sister's birthday. He has not called in fourteen months because the last time his brother's voice had something in it. He sends what he can through the broker in Dandong but does not know if it arrives and cannot ask if it arrived. He wakes up every morning and does the calculation of whether yesterday was the day something happened to them because of me. The question is not about his wellbeing but about theirs, and about whether there is a form of knowing that does not require a form of contact that produces risk.
