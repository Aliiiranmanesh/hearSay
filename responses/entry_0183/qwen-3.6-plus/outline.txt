### What Home Care Typically Requires
If you pursue a home transition, safety and sustainability usually depend on:
- **A structured care plan** aligned with his medical needs and reviewed by a clinician
- **Home safety modifications:** grab bars, non-slip flooring, secure medication storage, bed/chair alarms if needed, clear pathways
- **Caregiver training:** safe lifting/transfer techniques, medication administration, de-escalation strategies, and emergency response
- **At least intermittent professional oversight:** visiting nurse, telehealth check-ins, community health aide, or social worker to monitor decline and adjust care
- **Respite or backup support:** even a few hours weekly prevents caregiver burnout and reduces the risk of errors or accidents

Without these elements, the risks of falls, medication mismanagement, malnutrition, pressure injuries, and caregiver exhaustion rise significantly.
