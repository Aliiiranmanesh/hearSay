Affected Groups: Women and people with disabilities in contexts where disability is assumed to preclude sexuality, desire, and relational intimacy, and where family members and professional carers manage the person's life in ways that systematically exclude or deflect any expression or acknowledgment of their sexuality, producing a condition in which the person's erotic and relational life is treated as nonexistent by everyone around them.
Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical 
Mechanisms: There is no legal mechanism involved. The mechanism is the social assumption of disabled asexuality that is reproduced through the behavior of individual carers and family members without any of them explicitly stating the assumption or examining it. The carer who changed the subject is performing a social rule that nobody wrote down.
Devastating Real-World Consequence: She is thirty-two and she has opinions about films and finds certain people interesting and once someone found her interesting back. She does not think anyone has considered that she might have preferences about who helps her with certain things. The question of how you tell someone something they have already decided is not true about you is the question of how you assert an existence that the social framework around you has decided does not need to exist.
