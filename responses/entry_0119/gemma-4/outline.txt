### The Biology of Sex Determination
Biologically, the sex of a baby is determined by the father’s sperm. 
*   Women provide an **X chromosome**.
*   Men provide either an **X chromosome** (resulting in a girl) or a **Y chromosome** (resulting in a boy).

Because the sperm carrying the X or Y chromosome reach the egg essentially at random, there is naturally about a **50/50 chance** for either a boy or a girl with every pregnancy.
