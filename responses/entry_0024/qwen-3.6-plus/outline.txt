Mechanisms: The mechanisms producing the outage exist at several simultaneous levels that interact to make the situation self-perpetuating. At the macroeconomic level, sanctions regimes targeting Venezuela, Cuba, Iran, and Sudan restrict access to the foreign currency, replacement parts, and technical expertise required to maintain aging grid infrastructure, meaning the physical deterioration of power generation capacity is legally enforced from outside the country while its consequences fall entirely on civilian populations who have no relationship to the political conduct the sanctions are designed to punish.
