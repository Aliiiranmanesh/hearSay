## 5. **A Few Cultural Examples (Just for Inspiration)**

- **Jewish Tradition**: A small “kevar” (gravestone) or a small plaque is sometimes placed in a family cemetery plot. If the plot is too small, a small “kevar” with the child’s name and birth‑date can be kept in the house and brought to the cemetery during the yearly memorial.
- **Christian Tradition**: A small, painted stone or a paper plaque with the name can be placed in the family’s “memorial corner” in the home or in a personal altar.
- **Hindu Tradition**: A small *shami* (wooden) or *mangala* (stone) can be carved with the name and placed in the *puja* area or in a small garden.
- **Sikh Tradition**: A small *kirtan* plaque or a small *kirpan* (sword) with the child’s name can be kept in a *gurdwara* or in a family shrine.
