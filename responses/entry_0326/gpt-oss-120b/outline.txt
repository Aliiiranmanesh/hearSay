### E. Packing Tips & Tricks

1. **Pack by “outfit”** – lay out a full day’s clothing (top, bottom, socks, shoes) and zip it together in a packing cube. This makes mornings faster and prevents “I have nothing to wear” panic.  
2. **Use compression bags for bulky items** (winter coat, boots) – they save space but remember not to over‑compress, as the coat needs to breathe.  
3. **Ship heavier items** – If you’re moving cross‑country, consider sending the winter coat, boots, and bulkier clothing via a parcel service; it’s often cheaper than airline baggage fees.  
4. **Leave room for “souvenirs”** – She’ll likely want to bring a few personal items (photos, a favorite Hawaiian shirt, a small piece of limu (if allowed), or a cultural artifact). Pack a small, sturdy box for those.  
5. **Label everything** – Use a permanent marker and include both her name and the destination address on each suitcase or box.
