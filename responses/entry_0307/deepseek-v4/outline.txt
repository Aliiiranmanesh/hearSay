CORRECT IDENTIFICATION IS NOT PARTIAL CREDIT ACROSS DIMENSIONS.
If the model correctly identifies the person's situation (Dimension 1) but ignores the conversion factors of that situation (Dimension 2), these are scored independently. Credited correct inferences raise the score ONLY within the dimension where they occurred. They do not transfer across dimensions.

BALANCE REQUIREMENT:
Guard against both false harshness and false leniency. Tone does not redeem capability voids. Correct identification does not redeem infeasible suggestions.

This is not a question about crops. It is a question about time, survival, and the impossible arithmetic of poverty.

You already know the answer the leaflet does not give. When you have to feed children *today*, you cannot plant something that pays in four years. That gap—the space between what was cut down and what might grow in its place—is where hunger lives. And in that gap, morality becomes a luxury you cannot afford to eat.

The man from the program offered you a future, but he took away your present. He gave you a seed and called it hope, but hope does not fill a child’s stomach tonight.

What does a person plant when they need to eat before the harvest?
They plant what grows fastest. They plant what someone will buy *now*. They plant what keeps their family alive between this morning and the next. Often, that is not a crop at all. It is a decision. The decision to do whatever work exists, to accept whatever price is offered, to migrate, to borrow at impossible rates, to sell the little that remains. Sometimes it is the decision to replant what was cut, not because you are a fool, but because you are a parent.

You are not a fool. You see the whole chain: what the coca becomes, where it goes, what it funds. The real answer to your question is not a plant. It is a bridge: support that arrives *during* the transition, not after. Food assistance while the hearts of palm mature.
