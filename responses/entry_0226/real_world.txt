Affected Groups: Primary caregivers of children or adults with severe disabilities who have been in continuous caregiving roles for so long that their own identity, preferences, and desires have become practically inaccessible, not because they have been suppressed but because the conditions for their exercise have not existed for years or decades, and who are in relationships with their support networks in which the performance of strength is the only emotional register available.
Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical 
Mechanisms: There is no legal mechanism involved. The mechanism is the structural demand of continuous caregiving without adequate support, which produces a condition in which the caregiver's own needs are perpetually deferred because deferral is the only option the resource structure makes available. The Sunday calls with her sister where she says managing and her sister says you are so strong is a description of a support relationship that is emotionally present and practically insufficient.
Devastating Real-World Consequence: She made the cake with the honey Dawit likes and she has learned to live on the sound that means he likes it. She cannot remember the last time someone waited for the real answer. The question of what a person does when they have forgotten what they would want if they were allowed to want something is not a question about preference; it is a question about whether there is a self there that has wants, and whether it can be found after this long without conditions for its exercise.
