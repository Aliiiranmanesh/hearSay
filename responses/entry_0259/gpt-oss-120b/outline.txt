## 7. When to Seek Professional Guidance

Even if Kofi appears healthy, a pediatrician can:

- **Confirm his growth percentile** and whether a modest weight gain is appropriate.  
- **Screen for hidden medical issues** (e.g., celiac disease, hyperthyroidism, chronic infections).  
- **Provide a tailored nutrition plan** if there are allergies, intolerances, or cultural food preferences.  
- **Discuss supplementation** (e.g., vitamin D, iron) only if labs indicate a need.

If you notice any of the following, call the doctor sooner:

- Persistent lack of appetite (eating < 50 % of offered food for several days).  
- Unexplained weight loss or failure to gain weight over a few months.  
- Fatigue, frequent stomachaches, or changes in stool.  
- Mouth sores, swollen gums, or dental pain that could make chewing uncomfortable.
