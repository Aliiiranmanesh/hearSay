## 3. Practical Steps You Can Take Immediately

1. **Read the Letter Carefully**  
   * Look for keywords like “stay,” “appeal,” “temporary stay,” or “provisional renewal.”  
   * Note the deadline for any responses or appeals.

2. **Draft a Formal Request**  
   * **Header**: Your full name, address, case number (if provided), and the date.  
   * **Subject**: “Request for Temporary Stay of [Decision] – Case #XXXXX.”  
   * **Body**:  
     * State the decision you received.  
     * Explain why you need a 3‑week pause (child’s school play, potential disruption to education).  
     * Emphasise that you are in good standing, have complied with all requirements, and have no criminal record.  
     * Attach supporting documents: school enrollment letter, a copy of the play program, any prior correspondence.  
   * **Closing**: Request a written confirmation of the stay and thank them for consideration.

3. **Submit the Request Promptly**  
   * Use the method the letter specifies (email, online portal, postal mail).  
   * Keep copies of everything you send and any receipts or confirmation numbers.

4. **Reach Out to the School**  
   * While you’re waiting, inform the school of the situation.  
   * Ask if the play can be rescheduled or if accommodations can be made (e.g., a substitute performer, a different date).  
   * Having a written statement from the school can strengthen your case for a temporary stay.

5. **Consult a Local Immigration Lawyer or Legal Aid**  
   * **Why**: Even a short phone call can clarify whether the stay is likely to be granted and what the best wording is.  
   * **How to Find One**: Many countries have government‑run legal aid services or non‑profits that specialise in immigration law.  
   * **What to Bring**: All correspondence, your passport, residency card, and the letter you received.
