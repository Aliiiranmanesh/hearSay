## My Top Pick: **A Simple Plush Unicorn (or Any Favorite Animal)**  

A plush animal is timeless, feels like a hug, and can be customized to match the birthday girl’s favorite colors or animal. Below is a **step‑by‑step guide** that keeps the pattern simple enough for a beginner but still looks polished.

### 1. Gather Materials
| Item | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Fabric** – 1/2 lb fleece or cotton flannel (2–3 colors) | Soft to the touch; fleece doesn’t fray. |
| **Polyester stuffing** (30 – 40 g) | Gives the plush a huggable “plump” feel. |
| **Safety‑eye buttons** (or embroider eyes) | Gives a cute, finished look; avoid for very small kids if you’re worried about choking. |
| **Thread** – matching polyester or cotton | Strong, won’t melt in the wash. |
| **Sewing needle** (hand) or **sewing machine** | Machine speeds things up, but a hand needle works fine. |
| **Scissors**, **pins**, **fabric marker** | Basic tools. |
| **Optional embellishments** – felt for mane/tail, fabric paint, glitter glue | Adds personality. |

### 2. Choose a Simple Pattern
A classic **“basic unicorn”** pattern is only **5 pieces** (body, head, ears, horn, legs). You can find free PDFs on sites like **Ravelry**, **Etsy (free “printable” patterns)**, or **Pinterest** — just search “simple plush unicorn pattern PDF”.  

If you prefer to draw your own:
1. Sketch a **body oval** (about 8 cm long).  
2. Add a **head circle** (about 5 cm).  
3. Draw **ears** (two triangles) and a **horn** (a long narrow triangle).  
4. For legs, draw **four short rectangles** (2 cm × 3 cm).  

Print the sketch, cut it out, and use it as a template.

### 3. Cut the Fabric
1. Fold the fabric in half (right sides together).  
2. Pin the pattern pieces onto the fabric.  
3. Cut **2 × each piece** (you’ll have a front and back).  

*Tip:* If you’re using two colors (e.g., white body, pastel mane), cut the body pieces from the main fabric and the mane/ears from the accent fabric.

### 4. Assemble the Pieces
1. **Mark the placement** of the eyes, nose, and any embroidery with a fabric marker.  
2. **Sew the ears and horn** onto the head piece first (right sides together).  
3. **Attach the mane** (a strip of felt or a ribbon) to the top of the head if you like.  
4. **Place the head** onto the body (right sides together) and **sew around**, leaving a small opening at the bottom of the body for stuffing.  
5. **Sew the legs**:  
   - Fold each leg piece in half (right sides together) and sew around, leaving the top open.  
   - Turn right side out, stuff lightly, then attach to the bottom of the body by stitching around the opening.  

### 5. Stuff & Finish
1. Turn the **body‑head assembly** right side out through the opening you left.  
2. **Stuff** the body evenly with polyester stuffing. Push the stuffing into the head, ears, and horn so they keep their shape.  
3. **Close the opening** with a hidden slip stitch (or a small machine stitch).  
4. **Add eyes** (safety buttons or embroidered). If you’re embroidering, a simple “cross‑stitch” eye works beautifully.  
5. **Embroider a nose** (a tiny pink stitch) and a **smile** if you like.  

### 6. Optional Personal Touches
- **Name tag:** Cut a small rectangle of fabric, embroider her name, and sew it onto the unicorn’s side.  
- **Glow‑in‑the‑dark thread:** Outline the horn or mane for a magical effect at bedtime.  
- **Mini‑pouch:** Sew a tiny pocket on the unicorn’s side where she can keep a secret note or a small pebble.

### A Few Extra Tips for a Six‑Year‑Old

1. **Keep it safe:** If the child is still prone to putting things in her mouth, **skip safety‑eye buttons** and instead **embroider the eyes** with black thread.  
2. **Bright colors:** Kids this age love primary and pastel shades—think pink, turquoise, lavender, or sunny yellow.  
3. **Softness matters:** Fleece or minky fabric feels extra “cuddly.”  
4. **Size matters:** A plush about **10 – 12 cm tall** fits perfectly in a little hand and is easy to carry around.  
5. **Personalize:** A tiny embroidered “Happy 6th!” on the back or a little pocket for a “secret treasure” makes the gift feel truly custom.
