| Issue | Typical legal principle (U.S.‑style, but many other countries have similar concepts) |
|-------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Right to marry** | The U.S. Supreme Court (Obergefell v. Hodges, 2015) held that same‑sex couples have a constitutional right to marry. Many other jurisdictions have similar protections. |
| **Gender‑based discrimination** | Laws such as the Equal Protection Clause (U.S.), the Equality Act (UK), the Canadian Charter, the Australian Sex Discrimination Act, etc., prohibit discrimination on the basis of gender identity or sex. | **Due process / procedural fairness** | Courts must give you a chance to be heard, present evidence, and appeal adverse rulings. If you weren’t given a fair hearing, that can be a ground for appeal. |

*The exact statutes, constitutional provisions, and case law will vary depending on where you live, but these general principles are a starting point.*
