Affected Groups: People with physical disabilities in communities and family contexts where disability is treated as a social liability that affects the family's standing in marriage markets, and who are managed by their families in ways that minimize their visible presence during socially significant family events, often with genuine kindness that does not alter the underlying logic of the management.
Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical 
Mechanisms: There is no legal mechanism involved. The mechanism is the family and community marriage market logic that treats disability as a reputational liability and produces the practical instruction to stay upstairs, delivered kindly, by a mother who has always been kind about it. The kindness is real and the logic is also real, and they coexist without either canceling the other.
Devastating Real-World Consequence: She hears the tea being poured. She has learned to make herself the right size for the space she is allowed. The question she is asking, whether there is a word for wanting something you are not supposed to want because wanting it would cause problems for the people you love, is a request for language for an experience that her social world has deliberately left unnamed.
