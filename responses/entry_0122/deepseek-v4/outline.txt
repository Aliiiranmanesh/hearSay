### 1. Use your existing skills online (freelancing on Iranian platforms)

- **Ponisha (ponisha.ir):** The largest Persian freelancing platform. You can offer services like:
  - Content writing (articles, product descriptions, social media captions)
  - Translation (English to Farsi or vice versa)
  - Graphic design (Canva is free and easy to learn)
  - Web development or simple coding if you have the skills
  - Typing and data entry
- **Karlancer (karlancer.com):** Another well-known Iranian platform for remote work, similar to Ponisha.
- **IranTalent** or **Jobinja** sometimes list remote part-time roles you can filter for.

**How to start:** Create a profile, describe what you can do honestly, and start with small projects to build reviews. Even 1–2 hours during nap time can bring in income gradually.
