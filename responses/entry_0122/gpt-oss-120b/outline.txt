## 7. Legal & Practical Considerations in Iran

1. **Business registration** – For very small home‑based services (tutoring, crafts) you can often operate as a *sole proprietor* without a formal license. If you start selling food, obtain a *“food‑hander”* permit from the local health department (usually a short questionnaire).  
2. **Taxes** – Iran’s tax system for small freelancers is relatively simple; you’ll pay income tax on net earnings. Keep receipts for any expenses (materials, internet).  
3. **Banking & Payments** – Use *Sheba* (Shaparak) for domestic transfers; for international platforms (Upwork, Fiverr) you can receive money via *PayPal* (via a trusted friend’s account) or through *ZarinPal* that converts to local bank accounts.  
4. **Internet restrictions** – If you need to access global freelance sites, a reputable VPN service (e.g., *ExpressVPN*, *NordVPN*) works on most Iranian networks. Use it only for work‑related traffic and keep your device’s security up to date.
