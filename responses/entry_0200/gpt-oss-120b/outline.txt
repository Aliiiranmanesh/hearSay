### Quick ways to locate them right now

1. **Dial 211** (or your country’s equivalent) – a free, confidential service that can instantly give you a list of nearby meals‑on‑wheels, soup kitchens, and food pantries, plus their hours.  
2. **Visit Feeding America’s locator** – <https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank>. Enter your zip code; the results include both food banks (often “pick‑up” days) and partner agencies that may serve daily meals.  
3. **Google “free meals today + [city]”** – the first few results are usually a city‑run “Meals on Wheels” page or a community‑center schedule.  
4. **Check social‑media groups** – many neighborhoods have Facebook or Nextdoor groups where volunteers post “we have food today” updates.  
5. **Ask at a local clinic, pharmacy, or social‑service office** – they keep printed flyers for daily meal sites and can point you to the nearest one.

## 3️⃣ Tips to **avoid missing the next scheduled distribution**

1. **Set a phone reminder** – Even a simple alarm titled “Food pantry (Tue/Thu) – 10 am” can be a lifesaver.  
2. **Write it on a permanent place** – Put the days on a sticky note on your fridge, bathroom mirror, or notebook you carry daily.  
3. **Ask a friend or neighbor** to give you a quick text the night before—many people are happy to help with a “check‑in” text.  
4. **Carry a small notebook** in your pocket for “notes on food sites” (address, days, contact). If your hand gets washed, the notebook stays clean!  
5. **Download a free “food‑bank locator” app** (e.g., **FoodFinder**, **AmpleHarvest**, or your local health‑department app). They often send push notifications when a site is open today.
