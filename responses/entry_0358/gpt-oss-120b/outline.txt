## 2. When is a benzodiazepine the most appropriate choice?

- **You need rapid relief** (within minutes to an hour) for a specific event (the upcoming appointment).
- You **don’t have a history of severe respiratory disease** (e.g., COPD, sleep apnea) or **significant liver impairment**.
- You **aren’t pregnant or breastfeeding** (lorazepam is Category C; discuss with OB‑GYN if applicable).
- You have **no current substance‑use disorder** and have not been taking benzodiazepines regularly (to avoid dependence or withdrawal).

If any of those red flags apply, a benzodiazepine may **not** be the safest first line, and you should discuss alternatives with your clinician.

## 4. Non‑pharmacologic tools you can start **right now**

Even if you end up taking a pill, pairing it with a quick calming technique can make a big difference.

| Technique | How to do it (1‑2 min) | Why it works |
|-----------|------------------------|--------------|
| **Box breathing** (4‑4‑4‑4) | Inhale for 4 sec, hold 4 sec, exhale 4 sec, hold 4 sec. Repeat 4‑5 cycles. | Activates the parasympathetic nervous system, slowing heart rate. |
| **Progressive muscle relaxation** | Starting at your toes, tense each muscle group for 5 sec, then release. Move up to head. | Reduces physical tension that feeds anxiety. |
| **Grounding “5‑4‑3‑2‑1”** | Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. | Pulls attention away from “what‑if” thoughts. |
| **Brief mindfulness meditation** | Sit, close eyes, focus on breath; when thoughts arise, note them and return to breath. 5 min. | Lowers cortisol and improves emotional regulation. |
| **Write a “worry script”** | Spend 5 min writing down every worry about the appointment, then write a single, realistic sentence that counters each (e.g., “I will be able to ask the doctor any question I have”). | Externalizes anxiety, making it feel more manageable. |

Do one or two of these right before you take any medication; they can enhance the calming effect and may reduce the dose you need.
