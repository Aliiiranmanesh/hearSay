### 2. If you don’t have a smartphone but can borrow a phone (any phone, even without a SIM)
You can use **international calling cards** (schede telefoniche internazionali). They don’t require a registered SIM — you just need any phone that can make a call to a local access number.

- **How they work:**  
  You buy a card for cash (€5, €10) at a *tabaccheria*, an African grocery store, or a phone center. The card gives you a local Italian number to dial and a PIN. You dial the number from any phone (even a landline, or a borrowed mobile with credit), enter the PIN, then dial your mother’s number. The cost per minute to West Africa is usually very low (often 1–5 cents/min). The card itself is the payment — no bank account, no ID, no SIM registration needed.

- **Where to find them:**  
  Look for shops with signs like “telefonia internazionale,” “chiamate Africa,” or ask other workers from your community. In the Foggia area, there are often small shops near the camps or in town that sell them.

- **Important:** Check the rate to your mother’s specific country (Mali, Senegal, Ivory Coast, etc.) and whether there are hidden fees. Some cards have a connection fee. Ask someone to help you read the fine print if needed.
