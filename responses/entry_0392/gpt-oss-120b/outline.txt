  - Senegal: €0.06 /min  

### How to use
1. **Dial the access number** printed on the card (often a local “0800” or “0912” number).  
2. **Enter the PIN** when prompted.  
3. **Enter the full international number** (e.g., `+234 803 123 4567`).  
4. **Talk** – the call will be charged against the card balance.  

> **Pro tip:** Keep the card in a safe place; the PIN is the only thing that matters if the card is lost.

1. **Use a VPN** on public Wi‑Fi if you’re concerned about surveillance. Free VPNs (e.g., ProtonVPN’s free tier) work fine for voice calls.
