### A practical plan for quitting the videos

1. **Block access**
   - Use website/app blockers.
   - Remove private browsing if possible.
   - Keep your phone away from your bed.
   - Don’t stay alone with internet when you are weak.

2. **Know your trigger time**
   Most people fall when they are:
   - alone,
   - bored,
   - stressed,
   - awake late at night,
   - using social media.

   Protect those moments before they happen.

3. **When the urge comes, delay**
   Say: “I will wait 10 minutes.”  
   Then stand up, make wudu, walk outside, pray 2 rak‘ah, or call someone. Urges rise and fall like waves.

4. **Do not give up after relapse**
   Shaytan wants you to think: “I already failed, so I am hopeless.”  
   No. If you fall, immediately make tawbah and continue. A relapse is not the end.

5. **Replace the habit**
   You cannot only “stop.” You need replacement:
   - exercise,
   - Qur’an recitation,
   - useful videos/lectures,
   - learning a skill,
   - spending time with people,
   - fasting if possible.

6. **Find one trustworthy person**
   If you can, speak to an imam, counselor, older brother, or trusted friend. Addiction becomes stronger in secrecy.
