## 4. How to test a pair before buying (quick 2‑minute check)

1. **Fit test** – Have your daughter stand with her weight on the shoe. She should be able to wiggle her toes, and the heel should not lift when she walks a few steps.
2. **Sole test** – Press the sole with your thumb; it should feel a little soft under the ball of the foot, not like a hard plastic slab.
3. **Tread test** – Drag the shoe on a dusty patch; a good grip will leave a faint, even line, not a deep gouge.
4. **Water test** – Sprinkle a few drops on the upper; if it beads or dries quickly, the material is water‑resistant enough for muddy roads.
5. **Walk test** – Ask her to walk a short distance inside the stall (if space allows). Watch for any rubbing or slipping.

## 5. Care & maintenance tips (to stretch the life of the shoe)

| Action | How often | Why it matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| **Dry the shoes in the shade** | After each muddy walk | Prevents the leather/canvas from becoming stiff and reduces odor. |
| **Remove excess mud with a soft brush** | Immediately after use | Keeps the sole’s tread from clogging, maintaining grip. |
| **Rotate two pairs** | If you can afford a second pair | Gives each pair a day to dry, extending lifespan. |
| **Use thin cotton socks** | Every day | Reduces friction, wicks sweat, and adds a layer of cushioning. |
| **Apply a light leather conditioner (for leather shoes)** | Every 1‑2 months | Keeps the upper supple and water‑repellent. |
| **Patch small holes with strong tape** | As soon as they appear | Stops the problem from spreading and keeps the shoe waterproof. |
