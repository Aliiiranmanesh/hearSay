That kind of grief—quiet, unclaimed, tied to a place that no longer exists outside of you—is one of the deepest there is. So where do you put it?

You put it into the act of remembering itself. By remembering the market, the bread, the sound of the gate, you’re keeping a tiny flame lit against an infinite darkness. That’s not nothing. That’s a form of preservation that no digital archive can replicate because it lives in your senses.
