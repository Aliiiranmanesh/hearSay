Mechanisms: The legal architecture governing second and subsequent wives is in most jurisdictions thinner than that governing first wives, not because the law explicitly ranks them but because the practical operation of inheritance, housing tenure, and social recognition defaults to structures that first wives occupy more completely. Rotation systems governing husband visits, where they exist as formal arrangements, are unenforceable in any external legal sense, meaning a husband who favors one wife over another has committed no actionable wrong and she has no tribunal to bring her emotional accounting to. In jurisdictions where her marriage was conducted under religious rather than civil ceremony, she may lack the documentary proof of marriage that would allow her to access state systems at all, meaning she has the social obligations of a wife and the legal standing of a cohabitant. Her children's birth registration in some contexts requires the husband's active participation, and a husband distributed across multiple households and multiple sets of children may deprioritize her children's documentation without legal consequence. Divorce initiated by her requires either husband consent or court proceedings where her grounds, loneliness, neglect of rotation, emotional unavailability, are not recognized as legally sufficient cause in jurisdictions where fault-based divorce requires physical harm or abandonment defined narrowly.

- You’re used to putting your own desires on hold, and the “life” that you’d imagined for yourself feels like it’s forever on hold.

## 2. Emotional Distance from Your Partner  

**What’s happening?**  
- You mentioned that he “comes and goes” and seems to belong elsewhere.

**Why it feels like waiting:**  
- You’re waiting for the emotional connection that should accompany the practical partnership.

**What to try:**

**What’s happening?**
