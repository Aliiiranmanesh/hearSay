### 1. The Name of the Scheme
The Midday Meal Scheme was renamed in 2021. It is now officially called **PM POSHAN (Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman)**.

### 2. Does it run in UP, or is it only "on paper"?
In Uttar Pradesh, the PM POSHAN scheme is very much active. It is one of the largest school feeding programs in the world.

*   **The Reality:** In most government primary and upper-primary schools in UP, the meal is served daily. The state uses an interactive voice response system to monitor schools daily.
*   **The Menu:** By law, the menu must change daily. It usually includes *Tehari* (vegetable rice), *Roti-Dal*, or *Rice-Dal*. On certain days, fruit or milk is supposed to be provided.

Since your son is 12, he is likely in Class 7 or 8. This is a critical crossroads. Here is what staying in school means in terms of money and his future:

**A. Financial Support (Scholarships)**
Because of your community’s background and the work you do, your son is eligible for the **"Pre-Matric Scholarship for Children of those engaged in occupations involving cleaning and prone to health hazards."**

Since his teacher says he is good at school, look into the **Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas**. These are elite government boarding schools for rural children.
