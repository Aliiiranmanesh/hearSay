### 1. **Grief Has No "Right" Form**
There’s no universal template for how to mourn or what to do after a loss. Some people find comfort in rituals, others in solitude or creative expression (like writing, art, or music). ### 3. **Grief Can Be Messy and Non-Linear**
Grieving doesn’t follow a neat path or timeline. You might cry one day, feel numb the next, and then experience sudden waves of emotion weeks later. That’s normal. What matters is that you allow yourself to feel what arises, without judgment.

### 4. **Healing Can Coexist with Grief**
You don’t have to “move on” from loss to be okay. Healing doesn’t mean forgetting or being unaffected — it means learning to carry the grief in a way that allows you to keep living, loving, and connecting with others.
