## 4️⃣ Informal “Hawala” / Trusted Courier (only if you have a truly reliable person)

You already experienced a **12 % loss** with a courier you didn’t fully trust. That’s a classic warning sign: **informal channels are unpredictable and can be exploited**.  

If you ever need to use a courier again, follow these safeguards:

1. **Write a short contract** (even on a piece of paper) that states: amount given, exchange rate, and deadline for delivery. Both parties sign.  
2. **Use a *known* exchange rate** from a reputable source (e.g., the central bank’s RUB/KZT rate on that day) and attach a screenshot.  
3. **Insist on a **receipt** from the courier when they hand the cash to the recipient.
