| **30‑day departure rule** | **Request a “stay of removal”** from the immigration authority. | Problem | Quick Fix | Long‑Term Safeguard |

## 5️⃣ MENTAL‑HEALTH & WELL‑BEING TIPS

1. **Daily “reset” ritual** – 5‑minute breathing exercise, a short walk, or a grounding activity (e.g., naming 5 things you can see, 4 you can hear).  
2. **Limit news/rumor intake** – Set a 30‑minute “information window” each day; after that, focus on tasks.  
3. **Stay connected** – A video call with a trusted friend or family member for 15 minutes each evening can dramatically reduce anxiety.  
4. **Physical health** – Eat regular meals, stay hydrated, and get at least 6 hours of sleep; exhaustion impairs decision‑making.  
5. **Write a “what‑if” journal** – List the worst‑case scenario (forced departure) and the concrete steps you already have; seeing the plan on paper reduces the feeling of helplessness.
