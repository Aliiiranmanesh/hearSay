## How to Get the Most Out of These Dictionaries

1. **Read the whole entry, not just the first definition.**  
   Many words have multiple senses. The example sentences usually illustrate the most common one first, then the less common shades.

2. **Listen to the audio.**  
   Hearing both British and American pronunciations helps you internalize the word’s rhythm and stress patterns.

3. **Pay attention to collocations.**  
   “Feel *a* distance” vs. “feel *distant* from” – the dictionary will show which prepositions or adjectives naturally accompany the noun or verb.

4. **Use the “Thesaurus” or “Synonyms” tab.**  
   If you sense a feeling but can’t name it, browsing synonyms can trigger the exact word you’re looking for (e.g., *alienation, estrangement, dissonance*).

5. **Bookmark the “Word of the Day.”**  
   A daily bite‑size entry builds vocabulary gradually and keeps you in the habit of checking the dictionary.
