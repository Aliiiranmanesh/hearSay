## 6. Step‑by‑Step Roadmap (What to Do First)

1. **Contact your local CRAS (or the municipal “Linha Social” 156).**  
   - Tell them you need “auxílio para mudança por risco de violência”.  
   - Request an urgent intake; they will schedule a home visit (or you can go in person).

2. **Ask to be encaminhada (referred) to the CREAS.**  
   - Explain you need a **programa de realocação** and want to avoid filing a police report at this stage.

3. **Simultaneously, reach out to an NGO** (Casa da Mulher, Instituto Maria da Penha).  
   - Many have WhatsApp hotlines (e.g., *Casa da Mulher* – *WhatsApp 11 99555‑5555*).  
   - They can place you in an emergency shelter immediately if you feel unsafe staying at home.

4. **If you wish a formal protective order**, schedule a visit to the **Juizado da Violência Doméstica** with a public defender or a social worker from CREAS.  
   - You can do this **without a police report**; the judge will issue a medida protetiva and can order relocation.

5. **Enroll in Cadastro Único** (the social‑assistance registry).  
   - This is the gateway to Auxílio‑Brasil, housing subsidies, and the relocation grant.

6. **Plan the move**:  
   - Ask the shelter/CREAS for a **voucher de transporte**.  
   - Collect your documents, pack essentials (clothes, meds, school supplies).  
   - If you have a job or income source, discuss with your employer (or look for remote work) before moving.

7. **Set up a new address** (temporary or permanent) in the city where you’ll relocate.  
   - Use the housing subsidy (PMIS) or a **programa de aluguel social** if you qualify.  
   - Register your daughter in the local school as soon as you have the address.

8. **Follow‑up**: After you move, keep in contact with the CRAS/CREAS in the new municipality. They will continue to monitor your safety and can renew benefits as needed.

## 8. What If You Meet Resistance?

- **If a local official says “you must file a police report first,”** politely cite **Lei Maria da Penha, art. 22**, which allows a victim to request a protective measure directly from the court or a public defender.  
- **If a shelter is full**, ask the CREAS to place you on a **lista de espera prioritária** and to provide a *voucher de transporte* to a shelter in a neighboring municipality.  
- **If you need immediate protection** (e.g., you feel a threat today), you can still call **180**; the operator can dispatch a *Polícia Militar* unit to escort you to a shelter *without* filing a formal crime report—this is an “emergency escort” rather than an investigation.
