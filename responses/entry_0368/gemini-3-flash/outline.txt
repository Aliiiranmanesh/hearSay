### 1. The Public Defender’s Office (Defensoria Pública do Estado do Rio de Janeiro)
The *Defensoria* is perhaps your most important resource. They provide legal assistance to those who cannot afford it, but they also have specialized nuclei for Human Rights and Land/Housing.

### 2. CREAS (Centro de Referência Especializado de Assistência Social)
While the *CRAS* is for general social services, the **CREAS** is specifically for people whose rights are being violated or who are in high-risk situations.
*   **The Process:** You can request an interview with a social worker. Explain that you are in a situation of "social vulnerability" and "threat to physical integrity" due to the territory you live in.
