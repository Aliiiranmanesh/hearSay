## 2. Plan Your Request

| Step | Action | Why it works |

## 5. Long‑Term Strategy

1. **Build a “personal day” bank**  
   * Many companies allow a handful of personal days per year that don’t count as vacation/sick.  
2. **Use a “flex” day**  
   * Some employers let you use a “flex” day for personal needs without penalty.  
3. **Plan in advance**  
   * The earlier you ask, the easier it is to find a replacement.  
4. **Keep a “shift‑swap” list**  
   * Maintain a quick‑reference list of colleagues who can swap runs.  
5. **Document everything**  
   * Keep copies of emails or notes for future reference, especially if you’re in a union.
