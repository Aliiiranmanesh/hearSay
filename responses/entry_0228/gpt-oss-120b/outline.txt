## 4. The Body’s Clock – Resetting the “5:15” Wake‑Up

Your body still **anticipates** a workday. Here are a few gentle strategies to help it adjust without shocking the system:

1. **Light Exposure** – Open curtains or use a light‑therapy lamp soon after you finally wake up. Light tells the brain it’s daytime and helps shift the circadian rhythm.
