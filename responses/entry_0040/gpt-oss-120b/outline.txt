## 1️⃣ Diagnose the Card Issue First (so you won’t waste a trip)

| Possible Problem | Quick Test | Fix / Work‑around |
|------------------|------------|-------------------|
| **Expired or inactive loyalty/discount card** | Look at the expiration date on the back or log into the store’s website/app with the card number. | Reactivate online, call the store’s customer‑service line, or simply bring a photo‑ID and let the cashier apply the discount manually. |
| **Insufficient balance (gift card, prepaid card)** | Swipe the card at any register or use the store’s “Check Balance” kiosk (often near the entrance). | Add funds online, call the card‑issuer, or pay the difference with cash/another card. |
| **Card not linked to your account** | Try logging into the store’s app using the same email/phone you used to register the card. | Re‑link the card in the app or ask a staff member to “attach” the card to your profile. |
| **Magstripe/Chip malfunction** | Swipe the card at a self‑checkout kiosk. Does it read? | If only one swipe fails, try a different terminal. If all terminals reject it, the card may be physically damaged – request a replacement at the service desk. |

**Bottom line:** If the card is just a loyalty/discount card, you can usually still buy the clothing without it and apply the benefit later (most stores will credit the discount to your account after you present the card at the checkout or service desk). ## 2️⃣ Locate the Clothing Before You Walk In

### A. Use the Store’s Online “In‑Store Stock” Tool
1. **Go to the retailer’s website or app.**  
2. Search for the exact item (brand, style, color, size).  
3. Look for a filter or button that says **“Check Store Availability,” “Pick Up In‑Store,”** or **“See Nearby Stock.”**  
4. **Enter your ZIP code** (or allow the site to use your phone’s location).  
5. The system will show you which nearby stores have the item *and* the aisle/section number (many chains now show “Aisle 4, Shelf B”).  

> **Tip:** If the website only shows “in stock” without aisle info, note the SKU (stock‑keeping unit) or item number—it’s the number printed on the tag in the store.

### B. Call the Store Directly
- **Script to use:**  
  > “Hi, I’m looking for the [brand + style name] in size [your size] and color [desired color]. Could you tell me if it’s in stock at this location, and if so, which aisle it’s in? My loyalty card number is [xxxx] (if you want to confirm eligibility).”

- **Why call?**  
  - You get a real‑time answer (no “out‑of‑stock” surprises).  
  - The associate can **reserve** the item for you (most stores will hold it for a few hours or a day).  

### C. Bring a Printed/Screen‑Shot Reference
- Print the product page (or email it to yourself).  
- Highlight the **SKU / Item #** and **size/color**.  
- This visual cue is gold when you ask a staff member to “look it up” on the back‑room computer.

### D. Use a “Store Map” App (if the retailer has one)
- Some chains (e.g., Target, Macy’s, Nordstrom) have indoor navigation in their apps.  
- After you select the item, the app will plot a route from the entrance to the exact shelf.
