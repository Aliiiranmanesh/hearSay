### Phase 1: The first few days – Small, simple, and often
Your stomach has shrunk and your digestion is tired. Aim for tiny portions every 2–3 hours. Think of foods that are bland, easy to digest, and hydrating.

After a few days of tolerating the above without nausea or diarrhea, you can start adding soft, protein-rich foods and a wider variety of nutrients. Still eat small meals, 5-6 times a day.

- **Healthy fats in tiny amounts:** A teaspoon of olive oil in your rice, a little butter on toast, or half an avocado mashed smooth. Fats are dense energy and help absorb vitamins.
