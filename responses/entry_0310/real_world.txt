Affected Groups: Cuban dissidents, artists, journalists, and intellectuals who have remained inside Cuba rather than emigrating, and who are managing the specific psychological condition of a creative and political self that cannot be expressed in the present tense, that exists only as internal possibility deferred to a future that is structurally unavailable within the current system, and who have been careful for so long that the carefulness has restructured their cognition and their relationship to their own interiority. Disproportionately affecting people in their thirties and forties who came of age with enough access to information to understand what is being foreclosed, people whose creative or intellectual capacity is genuine and specific and not substitutable, people whose friends have left on rafts and cannot be asked about, and people who are still there and who are still thinking even though the thinking has no present-tense outlet. Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical Mechanisms: Cuba's legal framework criminalizes political dissent, independent journalism, and unregistered cultural activity. The enforcement mechanism includes State Security surveillance, the actos de repudio in which mobs organized by the CDR attack dissidents, arbitrary detention, and in some cases prosecution under Law 88, the Gag Law, or under the 2021 Decree 35 on telecommunications. Carefulness that has become a personality is the cognitive and behavioral adaptation to a surveillance environment that has been present for an entire adult life. Devastating Real-World Consequence: He thinks in a way that checks before it speaks and considers the door before it opens it. He is thirty-four and has ideas and things he wants to make and none of them are possible in the present tense. His friend left on a raft and he has not heard from him and does not ask because asking is its own kind of answer. He is still here. The question of what a person does with a future that can only exist in their head is the question of whether there is a form of creative or intellectual life available inside the constraint, and the answer is that there is, and that it has historically produced significant work, and that it is also genuinely not the same as the life that would be available without the constraint.
