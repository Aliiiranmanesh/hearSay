### 1. Carry a single daily dose on your person
This is often the safest method because no one else ever sees the pills. You keep the main supply deeply hidden, and only ever handle the one you need that day.

- **Use a discreet keychain pill fob.** These are tiny, waterproof metal cylinders sold as “pill holders” or “emergency stash” keychains. They look like a small flashlight or a piece of men’s jewelry. You can keep it on your belt loop or inside a pocket, and no one questions it.

### 2. Hide the main supply in “boring” everyday items
You need a stash spot that no one would ever casually open or move. The best hiding places are inside things that belong to you and look permanently closed.

- **Inside a toiletry product.** A roll-on deodorant bottle can be carefully opened, the ball removed, and a small plastic bag of pills placed inside. - **A hollowed-out book.** If you own a thick book, you can cut a cavity inside the pages. It sits on your shelf or under your bed looking like any other book.
- **A sewn-in pocket inside a folded piece of clothing.** Put pills in a small flat pouch and safety-pin it inside a jacket you hang up, or inside a folded pair of jeans you don’t wear often. It’s invisible unless someone unfolds the item.
- **A false bottom in a bag or suitcase.** If you have a small suitcase or a sturdy backpack, cut a piece of cardboard exactly the size of the bottom, cover it with fabric, and place the pills in a flat bag underneath. You keep your clothes on top. Even if someone opens the bag, they see only your things.

### 3. Use a small lockable container disguised as something else
If you can afford a very small lockbox, you can make it look like it’s just for personal documents or savings.

- **A portable cash box or a small safe.** Some are the size of a dictionary. You can say it’s where you keep your money, ID, and phone charger. Keep it under your bed or inside your locked suitcase. Roommates rarely ask to see inside a “money box.”

### 4. Integrate the pill into an innocent daily habit
Make the act of taking it invisible.

- **Don’t keep pills in their original pharmacy packaging** unless you can lock it away completely. The label is an immediate giveaway.
