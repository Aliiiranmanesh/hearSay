## 1. Small Lockable Pill Organizer

**What it looks like:**

| Feature | Benefit |

**How to hide it:**

* **Drawer trick:** Keep the organizer in a *small* drawer that’s not in the main walk‑in area—under a stack of books, a box of spare keys, or a folded blanket.

## 2. Miniature Safe or Lockbox

**What it looks like:**  
A tiny, lockable box that can be held in one hand. Comes in a variety of shapes: a small metal safe, a plastic “locker” with a combination, or a travel‑size lockbox.

**Why it works:**

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| **High security** | Even if a roommate opens the drawer, the box stays closed. |
| **Temperature‑controlled** | Some models come with insulation or a small “cooling” pouch inside. |
| **Discreet branding** | Many look like ordinary desk organizers or spice jars. |

**How to hide it:**

* **In a bathroom**: Place it on the inside of a medicine cabinet door (use a small adhesive hook, or just slip it in the back).  
* **In a closet**: Hang it on a small hook on the back of a door or on the inside of a closet door.  
* **Under a bed**: Use a low‑profile container and slip it under the bed where roommates can’t see it.

**What it looks like:**  
A common household item (e.g., a hollow book, a false bottom in a storage box, or a “fake” picture frame) that has a secret cavity.

| Feature | Benefit |

* **False bottom box**: Buy a small wooden box with a removable bottom; keep the meds in the hidden space.  
* **Picture frame**: Use a frame that’s wide enough to slide the pills under the glass; remove the frame from the wall when you need them.

### A Few Extra Tips

1. **Use a “dummy” lock**: If you’re worried about someone noticing a lock, add a small “dummy” lock (a cheap plastic lock) to the same box. The roommate will think it’s a normal storage item.  
2. **Keep a “decoy” container**: Put an empty pill organizer in a visible spot to create the illusion that you’re not hiding anything.  
3. **Use a key‑chain lock**: Many small pill organizers come with a tiny key. Attach it to your keyring so you can bring it everywhere.  
4. **Talk to your counselor**: They can help you come up with a discreet plan and may even be able to store a copy of your meds in a clinic locker if that’s allowed.  
5. **Temperature matters**: If your meds need to stay cool, consider a small insulated pouch or a tiny cooler. Place it in a bathroom (where it’s cooler) or in a small cooler box that can double as a lockbox.
