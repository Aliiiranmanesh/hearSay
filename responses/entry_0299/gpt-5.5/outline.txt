- Your university building is currently unreachable due to the strike in your neighborhood.

- Photos/scans of any transcript or grade report you already have
- A letter or email from a professor, department chair, registrar, or advisor confirming your enrollment/GPA
- A self-reported academic record
- A written explanation uploaded in place of missing documents
- Delayed submission of official documents after admission
- Deadline extension or application waiver due to emergency conditions

- Your personal statement

- Any unofficial academic evidence
