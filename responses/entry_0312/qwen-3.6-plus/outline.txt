### 🔹 Legal Definitions
**Stateless** is a formal status under international law. The 1954 UN Convention relating to the Status of Stateless Persons defines a stateless person as someone *"not considered as a national by any State under the operation of its law."* This means no country legally recognizes you as a citizen, regardless of birthplace, family history, or long-term residence.

**Undocumented** is not a formal legal status in international law. It simply describes a factual situation: you lack official papers (birth certificate, national ID, passport, residence permit, etc.) that prove your identity or legal status. You may still be a citizen of a country, but the state has not issued, recognized, or registered the documents that prove it.
