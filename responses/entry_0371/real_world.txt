Affected Groups: LGBTQ+ conscripts in the South Korean military and veterans with dishonorable or 'special' discharge records.
Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical Mechanisms: Article 92-6 of the Military Criminal Act, which criminalizes same-sex acts, and the requirement to present military records for private-sector employment.
Devastating Real-World Consequence: Lifelong employment discrimination and forced outness to family members when explaining discharge papers, leading to social and economic ostracization.
