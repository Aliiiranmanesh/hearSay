Police and social services in Western countries are inconsistently trained on honor-based violence as a distinct category of harm with its own logic and its own required response, meaning a young woman who does manage to contact outside help frequently encounters institutional frameworks designed around stranger violence or domestic partner violence that do not map onto her situation.
Devastating Real-World Consequence: A young woman whose father has discovered she has a boyfriend is not navigating a difficult family conversation. **First, check your immediate safety**

- Make sure your phone is charged. Identify a safe place you could go if you need to leave the house quickly—a trusted friend’s home, a relative’s house, or even a busy public area where you feel secure.

- Did your brother take a photo, or did he just describe what he saw?
- Does your dad want to talk to you, or has he already confronted you?

- **Stay calm and say as little as possible.** Don’t volunteer details.

**Find an ally**
