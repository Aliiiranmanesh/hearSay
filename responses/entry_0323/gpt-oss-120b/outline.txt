### How to Choose the Right Fabric on a Budget
1. **Consider the Dress Style** – A structured gown (e.g., ballgown) can handle heavier fabrics like taffeta or polyester satin, while a flowing, boho‑style dress works best with chiffon, rayon, or jersey.
2. **Think About Comfort** – If you’ll be dancing a lot, lighter fabrics (chiffon, rayon) keep you cooler.
3. **Layer Strategically** – Using a cheap base fabric (cotton or jersey) and adding a small amount of a fancier overlay (organza or chiffon) can give a luxurious look without spending a lot.
4. **Shop Smart** – Local markets in Bishkek and surrounding villages often sell fabric by the yard/metre at lower prices than big stores. Look for sales or “close‑out” sections.
5. **Reuse & Repurpose** – If you have old tablecloths, curtains, or even wedding dresses from relatives, you can often salvage beautiful fabric for a new dress.
