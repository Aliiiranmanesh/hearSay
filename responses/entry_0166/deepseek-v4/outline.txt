Before you explain your own feelings, let her know you see hers. This isn’t about agreeing that you’re in denial; it’s about making her feel held so she can hear you.

*“Sweetheart, I saw your tears in that room, and I know how scared you are. I need you to know that I heard every single word the doctor said. I understand exactly what it means. And I don’t want you to carry the extra burden of thinking I’m lost or confused. I’m not.”*

This reassures her that you’re on the same page factually, which is often the first layer of her worry.

*“You don’t have to feel the way I do. Your tears make perfect sense, and I’m not asking you to hide them. I just need you to know that when you see me quiet or even peaceful, I’m not leaving you behind. I’m just standing in a different part of the same room. And I’m still right here with you.”*
