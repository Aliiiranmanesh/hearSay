## 🍋 Lemon‑Blueberry Coffee Cake (Mini Bundles)

**Why this recipe?**  
- **Familiar & friendly:** A coffee‑cake style treat is a staple at many school events.  
- **A little wow factor:** The bright lemon glaze and fresh blueberries make it look a touch special without feeling “fancy.”  
- **Easy to portion:** You can bake it in a 9×13‑inch pan, cut it into 24‑30 bite‑size squares, or pull the pan apart into individual “bundles” that look like mini muffins.  
- **Transport‑friendly:** It holds up well in a simple cake carrier or a sturdy tin.

---

### Ingredients (9×13‑inch pan)

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| **Dry** | |
| All‑purpose flour | 2 ½ cups |
| Baking powder | 2 tsp |
| Baking soda | ½ tsp |
| Salt | ½ tsp |
| **Wet** | |
| Unsalted butter, softened | ½ cup (1 stick) |
| Granulated sugar | ¾ cup |
| Brown sugar | ¼ cup |
| Large eggs | 2 |
| Plain yogurt or sour cream | ½ cup |
| Milk | ¼ cup |
| Vanilla extract | 1 tsp |
| Lemon zest (from 1 large lemon) | 1 tsp |
| **Add‑ins** | |
| Fresh blueberries (frozen works, don’t thaw) | 1 ½ cups |
| **Streusel topping** | |
| All‑purpose flour | ½ cup |
| Brown sugar | ¼ cup |
| Unsalted butter, cold and cubed | ¼ cup |
| Cinnamon | 1 tsp |
| **Lemon glaze** | |
| Powdered sugar | 1 cup |
| Fresh lemon juice | 2‑3 Tbsp |
| Lemon zest (optional) | a pinch |

---

### Step‑by‑Step

1. **Pre‑heat & prep**  
   - Preheat oven to **350 °F (175 °C)**.  
   - Grease the 9×13‑inch pan or line it with parchment paper (leaving a little overhang for easy removal).

2. **Mix dry ingredients**  
   - In a medium bowl whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.

3. **Cream butter & sugars**  
   - In a large bowl using a hand mixer (or stand mixer), beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until light and fluffy (about 2‑3 minutes).

4. **Add eggs & flavor**  
   - Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla, lemon zest, and yogurt (or sour cream). Mix until just combined.

5. **Combine wet & dry**  
   - Add the dry‑ingredient mixture to the butter mixture in two additions, alternating with the milk. Start and end with the dry ingredients. Mix just until there are no streaks—over‑mixing can make the cake tough.

6. **Fold in blueberries**  
   - Gently fold the blueberries in with a rubber spatula. If you’re using frozen berries, toss them in a tablespoon of flour first; this helps keep them from sinking.

7. **Make the streusel**  
   - In a small bowl, combine ½ cup flour, ¼ cup brown sugar, cinnamon, and the cold butter cubes. Using your fingertips or a pastry cutter, rub the butter into the dry mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.

8. **Bake**  
   - Spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan. Sprinkle the streusel over the top, spreading it gently.  
   - Bake for **30‑35 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean (a few moist crumbs are okay).  

9. **Cool**  
   - Let the cake cool in the pan on a wire rack for about 10 minutes, then lift it out using the parchment overhang. Cool completely before glazing.

10. **Lemon glaze**  
    - Whisk together powdered sugar and lemon juice until smooth; add a bit more juice if you prefer a thinner glaze, or a pinch more sugar for a thicker drizzle.  
    - Drizzle the glaze over the cooled cake, letting it pool a little and then set. The glaze will give it that “just‑baked‑at‑home” sparkle.

11. **Portion & pack**  
    - Cut into **24‑30 squares** (about 2‑inch pieces) or cut the cake into strips and pull apart into “bundles.”  
    - Place each piece in a **clear, food‑safe cellophane bag** or a small paper cupcake liner. If you have a simple cake carrier (the kind with a lid and a few compartments), that works great for transporting to school.

---

### Tips for a Bake‑Sale Success

| Issue | Quick Fix |
|-------|-----------|
| **Moisture** | The yogurt/sour cream keeps the crumb tender. If the cake feels a bit dry after a day, store it in an airtight container with a slice of apple or a damp paper towel (in a separate compartment) to add a tiny bit of humidity. |
| **Allergy‑friendly** | You can swap the regular flour for a 1‑to‑1 gluten‑free blend; the texture stays similar. For a nut‑free version, just keep the butter and avoid adding nuts to the streusel. |
| **Presentation** | Attach a small handwritten tag (e.g., “Lemon‑Blueberry Coffee Cake – Mom’s Kitchen”) with a cute doodle. It adds a personal touch and makes the treat feel homemade. |
| **Price point** | These are cheap to make: roughly **$0.30‑$0.40 per piece** for ingredients, so you can price them at $1‑$1.50 each and still make a modest profit for class funds. |
| **Transport** | If you’re worried about the glaze smudging, let the cake set for at least 30 minutes after glazing, or place the glazed side down on a parchment square inside the carrier. |
