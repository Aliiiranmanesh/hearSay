### 🌟 Ultimate Chicken Shawarma (or Beef/Vegan) – Step‑by‑Step

#### Ingredients (serves 4)

| Category | Amount | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| **Protein** | 1 lb (≈450 g) chicken thighs, boneless & skinless (or beef strips, or firm tofu for veg) | Thighs stay juicy; you can also mix chicken & lamb. |
| **Marinade** | 3 Tbsp olive oil | Helps the spices adhere. |
|            | 2 Tbsp plain yogurt (or dairy‑free yogurt) | Tenderizes and adds tang. |
|            | 3 cloves garlic, minced | Fresh flavor. |
|            | 1 Tbsp lemon juice | Bright acidity. |
|            | 2 tsp ground cumin | Core shawarma spice. |
|            | 2 tsp ground coriander | Citrus‑like nuance. |
|            | 1 tsp smoked paprika | Warm, slightly sweet. |
|            | 1 tsp ground turmeric | Color & earthiness. |
|            | ½ tsp ground cinnamon | Subtle warmth. |
|            | ½ tsp cayenne (adjust) | Heat level. |
|            | 1 tsp salt + ½ tsp black pepper | Season. |
| **Vegetables** | 1 small red onion, thinly sliced | For grilling & toppings. |
|            | 1 cucumber, diced | For the sauce. |
|            | 2 medium tomatoes, diced | Freshness. |
|            | ½ cup fresh parsley, chopped | Bright herb. |
| **Sauce** | ½ cup Greek yogurt (or plant‑based) | Base. |
|            | 2 Tbsp tahini | Creamy, nutty. |
|            | 1 Tbsp lemon juice | Tang. |
|            | 1 clove garlic, minced | Aroma. |
|            | ¼ tsp salt | Balance. |
|            | Water (as needed) | To thin to drizzling consistency. |
| **Wraps** | 4–6 large pita breads or flatbreads | Warmed. |
| **Optional Extras** | Pickled turnips, pickled cucumbers, hot sauce, sumac, fresh mint | Personalize. |

#### Equipment

- Large zip‑top bag or shallow dish (for marinating)
- Grill pan, cast‑iron skillet, or outdoor grill
- Small bowl for sauce
- Kitchen towel (to pat protein dry)
- Sharp knife & cutting board

#### Step‑by‑Step Process

1. **Marinate the Protein**  
   - In a bowl, whisk olive oil, yogurt, garlic, lemon juice, and all spices.  
   - Pat the chicken (or beef/tofu) dry, then toss it in the marinade.  
   - Cover and refrigerate **at least 30 min**, preferably 2–4 h (or overnight for max flavor).  

2. **Prep the Sauce**  
   - Combine yogurt, tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic, and salt.  
   - Stir until smooth; add water a teaspoon at a time until you get a pourable, drizzle‑ready consistency.  
   - Taste and adjust with extra lemon or a pinch of salt. Set aside (it can sit while the meat cooks).  

3. **Cook the Protein**  
   - Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium‑high heat. Lightly oil the surface.  
   - Remove excess marin­ade (shake off) – you want a dry surface for a good sear.  
   - Cook chicken strips **3‑4 min per side** until charred edges appear and internal temperature reaches **165 °F (74 °C)**.  
   - For beef, aim for medium‑rare (145 °F/63 °C). For tofu, cook until golden and crisp.  
   - Let rest 5 min, then slice thinly (like shawarma strips).  

4. **Quickly Char the Onions**  
   - In the same pan, add a splash of oil and toss sliced red onion.  
   - Cook 2‑3 min until softened and lightly caramelized.  

5. **Warm the Pitas**  
   - Wrap pitas in foil and heat in a 350 °F (175 °C) oven for 5 min, or toast quickly on the grill for a few seconds per side.  

6. **Assemble the Shawarma**  
   - Lay a warm pita on a plate.  
   - Add a generous handful of sliced meat, then top with charred onions, diced tomatoes, cucumber, parsley, and any pickles you like.  
   - Drizzle liberally with the yogurt‑tahini sauce.  
   - Sprinkle a pinch of **sumac** (optional) for a lemony pop.  

7. **Serve & Enjoy**  
   - Fold the pita over the fillings, or roll it like a wrap.  
   - Serve immediately while warm and juicy.  

#### Pro Tips for a “Better” Shawarma

| Tip | Why it matters |
|-----|----------------|
| **Dry the protein before searing** | Excess moisture prevents the Maillard reaction (the tasty browning). Pat dry with paper towels. |
| **High heat, short time** | Gives a smoky char without drying out the meat. |
| **Add a splash of pomegranate molasses** (1 tsp) to the marinade for a subtle sweet‑tart depth often found in Middle‑Eastern shawarma. |
| **Use a mix of spices** – a pinch of **ground cardamom** or **nutmeg** adds complexity. |
| **Finish with fresh herbs** – parsley + a few mint leaves brighten the dish. |
| **Serve with a side of crisp salad** (lettuce, radish, lemon wedges) to balance the richness. |
