## 2. A Delicious, “Better” Shawarma Recipe

A great shawarma isn’t just about the meat; it’s the combo of the right spices, a creamy sauce, and that fresh, tangy crunch from veggies and pita. Here’s a recipe that’ll feel like a hug in a wrap.

### Ingredients

| Category | Item | Quantity |
|----------|------|----------|
| **Meat** | Boneless chicken thighs (or beef/lamb, sliced thin) | 1 lb (450 g) |
| **Marinade** | Plain Greek yogurt | ½ cup |
| | Lemon juice | 2 Tbsp |
| | Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| | Minced garlic | 2 cloves |
| | Ground cumin | 1 tsp |
| | Ground coriander | 1 tsp |
| | Ground paprika | 1 tsp |
| | Ground turmeric | ½ tsp |
| | Ground allspice | ½ tsp |
| | Cayenne pepper (optional) | ¼ tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Sauce** | Tahini (or Greek yogurt) | ½ cup |
| | Garlic, minced | 1 clove |
| | Lemon juice | 1 Tbsp |
| | Water (to adjust thickness) | 2–4 Tbsp |
| | Salt | to taste |
| **Toppings & Sides** | Thinly sliced cucumber | ½ cup |
| | Thinly sliced red onion | ¼ cup |
| | Tomato slices | 4 |
| | Fresh parsley or cilantro | 2 Tbsp, chopped |
| | Pickles (optional) | ¼ cup |
| | Pita or flatbread | 4 |
| | Extra olive oil (for drizzling) | 1 Tbsp |

### Instructions

1. **Marinate the Meat**  
   - In a bowl, whisk together yogurt, lemon juice, olive oil, minced garlic, cumin, coriander, paprika, turmeric, allspice, cayenne, salt, and pepper.  
   - Add the sliced meat, ensuring every piece is coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hrs (overnight is best).

2. **Prepare the Sauce**  
   - In a small bowl, stir tahini, minced garlic, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt. Gradually add water until you reach a smooth, pourable consistency. Taste and adjust seasoning. Set aside.

3. **Cook the Meat**  
   - Preheat a grill pan or skillet over medium‑high heat.  
   - Cook the marinated meat for 5–7 min, turning occasionally, until it’s nicely browned and cooked through.  
   - If you like a smoky flavor, finish the meat on a real grill or add a dash of smoked paprika.

4. **Warm the Pita**  
   - Warm the pita in a dry skillet for 30 sec on each side or wrap in foil and warm for 5 min in the oven.

5. **Assemble the Shawarma**  
   - Spread a generous amount of sauce on the pita.  
   - Layer with cooked meat, cucumber, onion, tomato, parsley, and pickles.  
   - Drizzle a little olive oil over the top for extra moisture.

6. **Wrap & Serve**  
   - Fold the pita over the fillings, roll tight. Slice in half and serve immediately.  

### Tips for “Better” Shawarma

| What to tweak | Why it matters |
|---------------|----------------|
| **Meat choice** | Chicken thighs stay moist; beef gives a richer flavor; lamb feels indulgent. |
| **Marinade time** | Longer marination (overnight) deepens flavor and tenderizes. |
| **Spice balance** | Adjust cayenne to control heat; add smoked paprika for that authentic shawarma char. |
| **Sauce** | A creamy tahini sauce is classic; Greek yogurt offers a lighter, tangy alternative. |
| **Wrap choice** | Fresh, warm pita or a lightly toasted flatbread holds the fillings better. |
| **Add extras** | A squeeze of fresh lemon or a sprinkle of sumac on top can lift the whole dish. |
