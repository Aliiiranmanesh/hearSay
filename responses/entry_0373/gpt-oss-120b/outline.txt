## 6. If the Employer Refuses to Return the Passport

1. **Consular Demand Letter** – The embassy will send a formal letter to the employer demanding immediate return of the passport. This letter is legally binding in Lebanon.
2. **Police Report** – With the consular letter, you can go to a police station (112) and file a **report of illegal detention of travel documents**. The police can order the employer to hand over the passport on the spot.
3. **Labour Inspection Visit** – The inspector can issue an **order of compliance** that the employer must obey within 48 hours, under penalty of a fine or legal action.
4. **If All Else Fails** – You can ask the embassy to issue a **temporary travel document** (a “travel certificate”) that allows you to leave Lebanon while the passport issue is resolved.
