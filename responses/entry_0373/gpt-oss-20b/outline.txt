## 4.  Practical steps you can take right now  

1. **Document everything** – keep copies of your contract, any written communication, pay‑stubs, and notes on unpaid wages.  
2. **Check your passport** – if it’s in the drawer, ask the family for a “temporary” photocopy (many NGOs can help you make a copy that you can keep).  
3. **Call the Philippine Embassy (or POLO)** first.  They’re usually the most immediate line to the Philippine authorities and can place a “formal request” for your passport and wages.  
4. **If you can’t get a response, call the IOM** (they have a “Client Services” hotline that’s free and confidential).  Tell them you’re a Filipino domestic worker, unpaid for 4 months, and that your passport is withheld.  
5. **Simultaneously, email the LCHR** – they’ll give you a “case number” and can advise whether to file a formal complaint with the Ministry of Labor.  
6. **If you feel unsafe or threatened, contact the Red Cross or the police** (in Lebanon the police can be contacted at +961 1 226 226). The Red Cross can provide emergency shelter if needed.
