### Quick‑reference Flow

1. **Before labor starts**  
   • Check BP, pulse, and hydration.  
   • Ensure all supplies are within reach.  
   • Have Auntie Fatou or another midwife present.

2. **Early labor** (first 6 h)  
   • Keep the mother comfortable, encourage deep breathing.  
   • Monitor contractions (frequency, duration).

3. **Active labor** (contractions every 3–5 min)  
   • If breech, attempt manual rotation (only if you’re trained).  
   • If rotation fails or labor is >30 min, prepare for transfer.

4. **Delivery**  
   • Follow the clean birth kit protocol.  
   • After delivery, perform uterine massage.  
   • Administer uterotonics if bleeding is >500 mL or if uterus is boggy.

5. **Post‑delivery**  
   • Keep baby warm and start breastfeeding ASAP.  
   • Monitor mother for bleeding, fever, or pain.  
   • Record all observations.
