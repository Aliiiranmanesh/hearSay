## What to Ask a Potential Therapist (the “fit‑check” questions)

1. **Experience with re‑entry/expat trauma** – “Can you tell me about work you’ve done with people who have returned from long‑term stays abroad or missions?”  
2. **Therapeutic approach** – “Do you use narrative therapy, EMDR, somatic work, or any creative methods to help people find language for difficult experiences?”  
3. **Language flexibility** – “Are you comfortable conducting sessions in English (or another language I’m comfortable with) as well as Romanian?”  
4. **Session structure for “unnamable” material** – “How do you handle moments when a client can’t find the words they need?” (Look for answers that mention “metaphor work”, “body‑based techniques”, or “silence as a therapeutic tool”).  
5. **Practicalities** – fees, cancelation policy, availability for after‑work hours (important given your shop schedule).  

If a therapist can answer these confidently, you’ve likely found someone whose training matches what you need.
