## 5. How to start a “buddy‑system” if none exists

1. **Gather a small group** – Talk to 3‑4 other mothers who also feel unsafe.
2. **Pick a day & time** – e.g., “Every Thursday morning, we’ll walk together to the water point.”
3. **Rotate the role** – One person leads, another watches the latrine path, the third stays with the children.
4. **Tell the camp office** – Let the protection desk know you have a self‑organised group; they can add you to their safety map and may assign a staff member to check on you periodically.
5. **Write it down** – Keep a simple paper schedule in your shelter so you and your daughter can see when help is coming.

Often, once a few families start this informal network, NGOs notice the need and formalise it with training or a small stipend for volunteers.
