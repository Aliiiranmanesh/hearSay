> “Mom, the fabric we chose was perfect, and I think it would be great for [Brother] to help me with the remaining selections—he has a good eye and I’ll still make the final call. This will let me focus on the décor while keeping everything on schedule. What do you think?”

A short, appreciative, and collaborative line like that does the job with **zero friction**.
