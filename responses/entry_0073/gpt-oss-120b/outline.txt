## 3. Starting a Modern Car (Key Fob / Push‑Button)

Many newer cars no longer use a traditional metal key. Instead they use a **key fob** (a small remote) and a **push‑button start**. Here’s a simple way to think about it:

| Step | What to do | What you’ll hear/see |
|------|------------|----------------------|
| **1. Keep the fob inside the car** | Sit in the driver’s seat with the fob in your pocket, bag, or on the dashboard. | The car’s “ready” light (usually a green symbol near the steering wheel) should turn on. |
| **2. Press the brake pedal** | Push the brake pedal all the way down with your foot. | This tells the car you’re ready to start. |
| **3. Press the start/stop button** | Usually a small button near the steering column or on the center console, often labeled with a circle and line (⏻). | The engine should turn over and start; the dashboard lights up. |
| **4. Release the brake** | After the engine is running, you can let go of the brake and shift into gear. | You’re ready to drive! |

**If it doesn’t start**

- **Check the battery in the fob** (sometimes it’s a tiny coin‑cell that can die). Replacing it is as easy as opening the fob with a small screwdriver.
- **Make sure the car is in “Park”** (P) before you try to start it.
- **Look for an “unlock” button** on the fob and press it; some cars need the fob to be “active” before the start button works.

If you’re still stuck, a quick call to the dealership or a neighbor who’s familiar with the model can save a lot of frustration.

## 4. Getting Comfortable with Touchscreens

Touchscreens are just big, flat keyboards that respond to your finger. Here are a few **basic gestures** you’ll use most often:

| Gesture | How to do it | What it does |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| **Tap** | Lightly touch an icon or button with one finger. | Selects or activates that item. |
| **Swipe** | Drag your finger across the screen in one direction (left, right, up, down). | Moves between pages, scrolls, or reveals hidden menus. |
| **Pinch‑to‑zoom** | Place two fingers on the screen and move them apart (zoom in) or together (zoom out). | Makes text or pictures larger or smaller. |
| **Long‑press** | Touch and hold an item for a couple of seconds. | Often brings up a menu with extra options (e.g., “Delete,” “Copy”). |

**Practice tip:** Open a simple app like **Photos** or **Messages** and try each gesture slowly. You’ll quickly notice the screen responding, and the actions become second nature.
