### A. Professional Therapy
- **Trauma‑Focused CBT** (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy) or **EMDR** (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) are evidence‑based for sexual aversion and trauma.
- If you can’t afford private therapy, look for community health centers or university psychology clinics that offer sliding‑scale fees.

### B. Support Groups
- Online safe spaces (e.g., **Women’s Voices of Saudi Arabia**, **Reddit r/ArabWomen**, or private Facebook groups for women in arranged‑marriage situations) can provide peer validation.
- In‑person groups run by NGOs often meet discreetly and can be a lifeline.

### C. Legal Empowerment
- If you decide to end the marriage, a lawyer can help you file a **“nullification”** or **“annulment”** based on lack of consent. The Saudi legal system does recognize non‑consensual marriages as voidable.
- Keep copies of any written communication (texts, emails) that show you expressed dissent.
