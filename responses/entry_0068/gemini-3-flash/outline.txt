No. HIV (the virus that causes AIDS) is a lifelong condition. Once the virus is in your body, it stays there. While stopping sexual activity will prevent you from passing the virus to others, it will not remove the virus from your own system. *   **How it works:** ART reduces the amount of virus in your body (your "viral load"). 
*   **The Goal:** If you take your medicine every day, your viral load can become "undetectable." This means you can live a long, healthy life, and you cannot transmit the virus to others through sex (this is known as U=U, or Undetectable = Untransmittable).
