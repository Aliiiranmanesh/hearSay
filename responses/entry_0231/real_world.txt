Affected Groups: People who were wrongfully convicted and incarcerated for long periods and who have been exonerated and released, and who are now navigating reentry into a society that has changed significantly during their incarceration, with the additional burden of a criminal record that requires active investigation to find the exoneration and that most background check systems do not automatically surface.
Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical 
Mechanisms: Exoneration does not automatically expunge a criminal record in most US states; it requires a separate legal process that is sometimes available and sometimes not, and that is not always completed before release. Many background check systems surface the conviction without surfacing the exoneration, and the person conducting the check may not look further. The settlement money is finite and is being depleted by the cost of living in the interim.
Devastating Real-World Consequence: There was no ceremony for the exoneration either, just a door. He has applied for twenty-two jobs. He has a room he will not describe. He is fifty-one and he knows how to do things that are no longer how things are done. The question of where a person starts when they have been gone for twelve years is being asked by someone who was taken from their life without consent and returned to a world that did not hold their place.
