## 8️⃣ Manage Your Finances While You Search  

1. **Create a basic budget** (income vs. expenses). Use a free spreadsheet or a paper ledger.  
2. **Prioritize essential bills** (rent, utilities, food, transportation).  
3. **If settlement money is low:**  
   - **Apply for SNAP (food stamps)** and **LIHEAP (energy assistance)** – these are often available regardless of criminal history.  
   - **Check eligibility for Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF)** or **General Assistance** in your county.  
4. **Open a low‑fee checking account** (many banks offer “no‑minimum‑balance” accounts; credit unions are often friendlier). Bring your ID and proof of address.  
5. **Avoid high‑interest payday loans**. If you need a short‑term loan, consider a **credit‑union emergency loan** or **family/friend** loan with written terms.

## 9️⃣ Keep Your Well‑Being in Focus  

- **Mental health:** Re‑entry can be stressful. Look for **free counseling** through community health centers or **reentry nonprofits**.

- **Support network:** Even if you “won’t describe” your living situation, try to stay connected with at least one trusted person (family, friend, case manager). Isolation makes the job hunt harder.
