## Why the Quiet Hours Exist  

1. **Respect for Local Communities** – Many campuses are embedded in residential neighborhoods; loud parties can disturb nearby residents.  
2. **Student Well‑Being** – Adequate sleep is critical for academic performance; enforced quiet reduces sleep deprivation.  
3. **Safety** – Reduced nighttime activity lowers the risk of accidents or crime.  
4. **Cultural Norms** – In many societies, nighttime is reserved for rest; universities reflect that cultural expectation.
