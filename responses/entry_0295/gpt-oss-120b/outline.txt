## 2. How to Set Up a Low‑Risk “Silent” Signal Right Now

Below is a step‑by‑step guide for the **missed‑call ping** (the most universally available and least likely to raise suspicion).

1. **Pick a time** you think she will be able to look at her phone (e.g., after dinner, 20:00 local).  
2. **Make the call** from your number.  
3. **Hang up immediately**—ideally after the first ring, before the call is answered.  
4. **Observe:**  
   - If she calls you back, that may be her way of confirming she saw the ping.  
   - If she sends a silent text or a single emoji after seeing the missed call, that can be her acknowledgment.  

**Why this works:** A missed call creates a log entry on the phone but no voice data. If someone were to listen in, they would only hear “a call was placed.” The act of hanging up quickly reduces the chance of a recorded conversation being captured.

### Optional “double‑check”

- **Second day, different pattern:** Call, let it ring twice, then hang up.  
- **Third day, same pattern but at a slightly different minute (e.g., 20:03).**  

If she follows the pattern back (e.g., she calls you back at the same time each day), both of you will have a **mutual, low‑tech heartbeat** without any content that can be read.

## 3. If You Want Something Even More Invisible

### Flash‑SMS (type‑0)

1. **Install “SMS Tools”** (Android) or a similar app that can send a type‑0 message.  
2. **Create a new message** → set the message type to “type‑0.”  
3. **Send** to her number.  
4. **Result:** Her phone receives a silent notification (often a vibration) but nothing appears in the inbox.  

*Note:* Not all carriers support type‑0. Test it on a friend’s phone first if you can.

### Encrypted “read‑receipt” trick (Signal)

1. **Send a short, harmless text** like “👍”.  
2. **Immediately delete the message for everyone** (press and hold → Delete → “Delete for everyone”).  
3. **Result:** If she has the app open, she will see the message appear and then disappear, but the read receipt (“double‑check”) will stay, confirming she saw it.

## 4. Safety & Surveillance Considerations

| Risk | What to Watch For | Mitigation |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| **Call‑log monitoring** | Authorities may request or intercept call logs. | Keep the call **short**, **infrequent**, and **at irregular times** (avoid daily patterns that could be flagged). |
| **SMS content scanning** | Even “empty” SMS can be logged. | Use **flash‑SMS** (type‑0) where possible, or rely on **missed calls** instead of text. |
| **App metadata** | Encrypted apps still expose metadata (who you contact, when). | Use **Signal’s “Sealed Sender”** mode (Settings → Privacy → Sealed Sender) to hide the sender’s IP. |
| **Device compromise** | Malware could read contacts or logs. | Keep the phone **updated**, install apps only from trusted sources, and consider a **secondary “burner” phone** for these signals. |
| **Physical surveillance** | Anyone watching you could see you dialing. | If you suspect someone is watching, **use a public phone** (e.g., a pay‑phone) or a **friend’s device** for the missed‑call ping. |
