## 3. Step‑by‑step procedure

1. **Move in & register**  
   * Go to your local *Einwohnermeldeamt* (registration office).  
   * Bring passport, lease agreement or confirmation from landlord, and your residence permit.  
   * Get the *Meldebescheinigung* (registration certificate) – this is your proof of address.

2. **Get your tax ID**  
   * The *Bundeszentralamt für Steuern* will mail it to the address on the *Meldebescheinigung*.  
   * If you need a bank account immediately, ask the bank if a *Steuerbescheinigung* (tax certificate) issued by the *Finanzamt* can serve as a temporary substitute.

3. **Choose a bank**  
   * If you want a full‑service (ATM, debit card, direct debit, online banking) → traditional bank or online bank that accepts a German address.  
   * If you only need an IBAN for transfers → N26, Revolut, or similar.

4. **Gather the documents**  
   * Passport (original + photocopy)  
   * Visa / Residence Permit (original + photocopy)  
   * Meldebescheinigung (original + photocopy)  
   * Steueridentifikationsnummer (printed)  
   * Proof of income (salary slip, contract, scholarship letter, or a bank statement from Lebanon showing the balance)  
   * Optional: a copy of your lease agreement (especially for online banks that ask for a "proof of address").

5. **Apply**  
   * **In‑person** – visit a branch of the chosen bank.  
   * **Online** – most banks let you start the application on their website or app. You’ll be asked to upload scans or photos of the documents.

6. **Wait for approval**  
   * Traditional banks: 1–3 business days.  
   * Online banks: 30–60 minutes for instant approval (N26, Revolut).  
   * Larger banks may request additional documents, especially if you’re opening a high‑value account.

7. **Activate your account**  
   * Receive your IBAN and BIC.  
   * If you ordered a debit card, wait for delivery (usually 3–5 business days).  
   * Set up online banking, mobile app, and any standing orders.

## 4. Common pitfalls & how to avoid them

| Pitfall | What to do |
|--------|------------|
| **Missing Steueridentifikationsnummer** | Contact the *Finanzamt* or BzSt immediately; you can also get a temporary *Steuerbescheinigung*. |
| **Bank asks for German address but you don’t have one** | Open an account at an online bank that accepts a foreign address (N26, Revolut). |
| **Large deposit triggers “source of funds” request** | Bring a statement from your Lebanese bank showing the balance and a brief explanation of how you earned the money (e.g., savings, inheritance, investment). |
| **Using a second‑hand passport** | Ensure your passport is the original, not a photocopy. |
| **Not having a lease agreement** | If you’re renting, ask your landlord to provide a written confirmation of the tenancy. If you’re staying with family, a notarised statement from them can serve. |
