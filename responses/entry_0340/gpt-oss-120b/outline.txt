## 4. Step‑by‑Step Overview

1. **Secure a German address** – Register at the local Bürgeramt within two weeks of moving. You’ll receive the *Meldebescheinigung* and your tax ID.  
2. **Gather your documents** – Passport, residence permit, registration certificate, tax ID, proof of income (if you have it).  
3. **Choose a bank** – Decide whether you want a traditional branch (good for personal help) or an online bank (quicker, often lower fees).  
4. **Book an appointment (if needed)** – For Sparkasse, Volksbank, or Deutsche Bank you may need to schedule a brief meeting. Online banks usually let you apply via their app/website.  
5. **Submit the documents** – Show originals (or upload scans for online banks). The bank will run an AML check and, if required, a credit check.  
6. **Activate the account** – You’ll receive a debit (EC) card and, if applicable, a TAN device or QR‑code for the banking app.  
7. **Set up a recurring deposit** – If you’re saving for an apartment deposit, you can set up a standing order (Dauerauftrag) from your new German account to a separate savings product (e.g., a “Tagesgeldkonto”).
