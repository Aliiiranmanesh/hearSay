## 3. How to Keep the Letter (and the Diagnosis) Private

1. **Digitize it securely**  
   - Scan the letter and store it in an encrypted cloud service (e.g., iCloud with two‑factor authentication, or a password‑protected PDF).  
   - Delete the physical copy if you’re worried about it being found.  

2. **Use a “medical vault”**  
   - Many banks, law firms, and even some online services (e.g., “MyMedicalVault”) let you keep medical documents in a locked, digital vault that only you (and anyone you authorize) can access.  

3. **Ask the clinic for a *patient‑only* copy**  
   - When you get a second opinion, request that the clinic give you a copy of the diagnostic report *without* the physician’s name or hospital letterhead. This can be used for future appointments without revealing the original letter.

4. **Set a “paper‑free” policy at home**  
   - Keep all medical paperwork in a single, locked drawer or a fire‑proof safe. If you have a partner, agree on a “no‑show” rule for outsiders (including in‑laws) when you’re at home.
