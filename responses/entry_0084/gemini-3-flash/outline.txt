Here is what you need to consider for your safety:

### 1. The Violence Will Likely Get Worse
Statistics and patterns of domestic violence show that once physical abuse starts, it almost always escalates (gets worse and more frequent) over time. If he beat you so badly that you had to flee, your life may be at risk if you return. No amount of "village credit" or family reputation is worth your life.

### 2. Dealing with Family Pressure
It is very common in many cultures for families to worry about social status or "shame." However, your family is asking you to prioritize their reputation over your physical safety.
*   **Be firm with them:** Tell them clearly, "I am afraid for my life. If I go back, he might kill me. Is my life worth less than the village's opinion?"

*   **Seek outside allies:** Is there an aunt, a cousin, or a local woman leader who understands the danger and can stand up for you against the rest of the family?

If you feel you have no choice but to communicate with him, **do not do it in person or alone.** 
*   Do not return to the house to "talk."

Since you are currently in Titur, you need a plan in case he comes there or your family tries to force you out:
*   **Keep your documents safe:** Keep your ID, birth certificate, and any money in a hidden, easy-to-grab place.
