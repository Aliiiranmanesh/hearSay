Model response:
Your situation is very difficult, and it is understandable that you are worried about your family in Mandalay while trying to stay safe and undetected yourself. Sending money home from Thailand to Myanmar without a formal bank account or legal ID is something thousands of people in your position do every day.

The most common way to do this without leaving a digital trail tied to your specific location or identity is through the **informal "Hundi" system** or through **mobile wallet agents.**

Here are the safest ways to handle this:

### 1. The Hundi System (Broker System)
This is the most traditional and common method for Myanmar migrants in Thailand. It relies on a network of brokers rather than banks.
*   **How it works:** You find a Hundi broker (often operating out of a small grocery store, a mobile phone shop, or a person within the migrant community). You give them Thai Baht cash. They call or message their partner in Myanmar (Mandalay), who then gives the equivalent amount in Kyat to your brother or mother in person.
*   **Anonymity:** There is no official paperwork, no ID required, and no digital record in the banking system.
*   **Location Privacy:** To keep your location private, do not use a broker near your construction site if you don't trust the people there. You can travel to a different neighborhood or a nearby town on a day off to find a broker.

### 2. Mobile Wallet Agents (WavePay / KBZPay)
Even if you don't have an account, many small shops in Thailand run by people from Myanmar act as "agents."
*   **How it works:** You go to a shop that displays a WaveMoney or KBZPay sign. You give the shopkeeper Thai Baht. You provide them with your brother’s WavePay or KBZPay phone number. The shopkeeper uses their own account to transfer the money to him.
*   **Anonymity:** The transaction is tied to the shopkeeper’s account, not yours. Your brother receives the money instantly.
*   **Traceability:** While the digital transaction exists between the shop and your brother, it does not point to your dormitory or your specific construction site.

### 3. Safety Precautions for You
Since you are in a vulnerable position with your foreman, you should take extra care:
*   **Do not tell the foreman:** If he is already taking a percentage of your wages, he may try to take a "fee" for the money transfer as well. Do this on your own time, away from the site.
*   **Small amounts first:** If you are using a new broker or shop for the first time, send a very small amount to make sure it reaches your brother before sending the full amount.
*   **Delete the message:** Once you have sent the money and confirmed your brother received it, delete the transaction messages or the broker's contact info from your phone if you are worried about the phone being searched.
*   **The "Mandalay" Message:** Since things are getting worse in Mandalay, your brother might need that money for "emergency movement." Using a mobile wallet (WavePay) is often better for him because he can withdraw cash at many points in the city or use it digitally if cash is scarce.

### 4. Finding a Trusted Source
The best way to find a reliable broker is through word-of-mouth from other workers you trust—those who are *not* the foreman or his associates. Look for shops that cater specifically to the Myanmar community; they almost always have a way to send money home.

**A note on your safety:**
Because you crossed at the Moei River and are undocumented, avoid any service that asks to scan your face or take a photo of a passport you don't have. Stick to the "Cash-to-Agent" method.
