Affected Groups: Tajik farmers and rural communities in the Fergana Valley sharing water resources with Kyrgyzstan.
Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical Mechanisms: Non-binding transboundary water sharing agreements (Aral Sea Basin) and lack of international arbitration for district-level infrastructure.
Devastating Real-World Consequence: Agricultural collapse leading to forced labor migration to Russia, often in seasonal extremes, to replace lost household income.
