**How to File a Complaint**  
1. **Collect evidence** – screenshots, timestamps, PDFs of documents you sent, any email threads.  
2. **Write a clear statement** – what happened, dates, what you’ve tried, and the impact (e.g., rent due).  
3. **Submit** – most regulators have an online form.  
4. **Keep a copy** – send yourself a PDF of the complaint.  

Regulators typically don’t fix the issue directly, but they can compel the company to respond and enforce compliance. If the company refuses, you can ask the regulator to investigate for potential regulatory violations (e.g., failure to process KYC, breach of GDPR, etc.).
