## 1. Leafy Greens – The go‑to for low light

| Veggie | Pot size | Light needs | Notes |
|--------|----------|--------------|-------|
| **Lettuce** (any variety) | 6‑8 in. pot | 4–6 h sun, prefers cooler temps | Grow in rows or “cut‑and‑come‑back” style. |
| **Spinach** | 6‑8 in. pot | 4–6 h sun, loves shade | Harvest leaves as they grow; faster than other greens. |
| **Arugula** | 6‑8 in. pot | 3–5 h sun | Quick turnover – 3–4 weeks. |
| **Kale** | 10‑12 in. pot | 4–6 h sun | Tolerates cold, so good for a balcony that stays cool. |
| **Swiss chard** | 10‑12 in. pot | 4–6 h sun | Colorful stems add visual interest. |
| **Bok choy / Chinese kale** | 8‑10 in. pot | 3–5 h sun | Very hardy, thrives in cooler light. |

> **Tip:** If the balcony gets hot in the afternoon, give the pots a bit of shade (a simple shade cloth or positioning them under a building) to keep the soil from drying out too fast.

## 2. Quick‑Harvest Root & Legume Options

| Veggie | Pot size | Light needs | Notes |
|--------|----------|--------------|-------|
| **Radishes** | 6‑8 in. pot | 3–5 h sun | Harvest in 4–6 weeks; great for beginners. |
| **Peas** | 10‑12 in. pot | 4–6 h sun | Use a small trellis or stake for support. |
| **Broccoli** | 12‑18 in. pot | 4–6 h sun | Needs a bit more depth; works best in cooler climates. |
| **Cauliflower** | 12‑18 in. pot | 4–6 h sun | Similar to broccoli; can grow in partial shade. |

> **Quick‑win idea:** Sow peas and radishes together—they’ll mature at opposite times, giving you a steady stream of harvest.

## 4. Microgreens – Tiny, Fast, and Sun‑Smart

Microgreens can be grown in a shallow tray (3‑4 in. deep) and will be ready to harvest in 7–14 days. They are perfect for a balcony with only a few hours of light, especially if you’re looking for something quick and nutritious. Try lettuce, radish, broccoli, or even kale microgreens.

## 5. Container Essentials & Care

| Item | Why it matters |
|------|----------------|
| **Good quality potting mix** (not garden soil) | Holds moisture and drains well. |
| **Drainage holes** | Prevent root rot. |
| **Slow‑release fertilizer** (or balanced NPK) | Feed every 4–6 weeks. |
| **Mulch (organic matter)** | Keeps moisture, reduces weeds. |
| **Self‑watering or drip system** | Helps if the balcony is hard to reach. |
| **Shade cloth or positioning** | Protects from afternoon heat. |
| **Grow lights (LED)** | Optional if you get <2 h of natural light. |

> **Watering rule of thumb:** Check the top inch of soil; if it’s dry, water. In hot, shaded spots, you may need to water daily; in cooler, shady spots, 2–3 times a week may suffice.

## 6. Quick Start Checklist

1. **Pick a plant** – start with something simple like lettuce or radishes.  
2. **Choose the right pot** – 6–8 in. for leafy greens, larger for broccoli/cauliflower.  
3. **Fill with potting mix** – add a handful of compost for nutrients.  
4. **Plant seeds** – follow the depth instructions on the seed packet.  
5. **Water lightly** – keep the soil moist but not soggy.
