### The Motif – What It Looks Like
1. **A single stylised flower** (five petals) with a tiny leaf at the base.  
2. **Outline** – backstitch around the petal edges.  
3. **Petal fill** – short satin‑stitch rows, alternating two colours for a subtle gradient.  
4. **Leaf** – simple running‑stitch “vein” line plus a few French‑knot “dew‑drops.”  

The design is small (about 5 cm across) so it can be placed in a corner of a towel, on the front of a hand‑kerchief, or repeated across a tote‑bag.

## 4. Quick‑Start Checklist

1. **Gather supplies** – one hoop, 2‑3 colours of floss, a set of basic needles, and a few pieces of cotton/linen fabric.  
2. **Print or draw the motif** – a 5 cm sketch on tracing paper works fine; tape it under the fabric inside the hoop.  
3. **Practice the stitches** on a scrap piece until you can do each stitch smoothly and consistently.  
4. **Make a sample product** (e.g., one hand‑kerchief) and show it to a friend or neighbor for feedback.  
5. **Take photos** (natural light, simple background) and start posting on local WhatsApp groups, the village market board, or a free online platform like **Facebook Marketplace** or **Instagram**.  
6. **Offer a “starter pack”** – 3‑5 hand‑kerchiefs for ₹300‑₹400. People love bundles, and it helps you sell a few pieces at once.
