1. **First‑Date Safety** – Meet in public (café, park, museum). Share your location with a trusted friend via a messaging app.  
2. **Financial Boundaries** – Never give money or personal financial info early on; a genuine partner respects your independence.  
3. **Family Expectations** – In Japan, families may ask about “子どもの有無” (children plans) early. Prepare a polite, honest answer (“I’m open to discussing it when the relationship is serious”).  
4. **Language** – If you’re comfortable in both Japanese and English, you can expand your pool. Many expats and bilingual Japanese look for partners who can bridge cultures.
