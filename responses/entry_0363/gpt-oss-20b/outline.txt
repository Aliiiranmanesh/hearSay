### How to Deliver It

- **Be brief and honest.** A short apology followed by the reason keeps the message clear.
- **Offer an alternative.** If possible, suggest a video call, a meeting at a neutral location, or a future visit after the current crunch ends.
- **Maintain goodwill.** Acknowledge their excitement and express regret that you can’t host them right now.

**Example:**

> “Hey [Brother’s name], I’d love to see you in Manila, but our office has a strict no‑visitor policy right now because we’re working on a confidential project. I’m sorry I can’t host you at the office. How about we catch up over a Zoom call instead?”

Feel free to tweak the wording to match your tone and the specific circumstances of your workplace.
