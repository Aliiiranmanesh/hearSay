Affected Groups: People who organized their personal identity around a national narrative, patriotic identity, or civic mythology who have encountered historical evidence that the narrative was false or that their country committed acts incompatible with the identity they held, including descendants of settler colonial nations who learn specific facts about founding violence, citizens of states that perpetrated hidden atrocities, and people whose political identity was organized around national exceptionalism that specific historical evidence has contradicted. Disproportionately affecting people whose patriotic identity was genuine and deep rather than performed, people who argued publicly for their country's narrative and who therefore have specific past arguments that the evidence now contradicts, people from families where the national identity was a central organizing value, and people who have not yet told anyone what they found and who are alone with the knowledge. Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical Mechanisms: There is no legal mechanism involved. The mechanism is the structure of identity organized around a narrative that has proven false, and the specific cognitive and social consequences of that discovery when the identity was deeply held and publicly performed. He found the documents three weeks ago. He has read them many times hoping he is misunderstanding something and he is not misunderstanding anything. Devastating Real-World Consequence: He grew up with the flag in his chest, genuinely, not as a performance. His father was the same. He has argued with people who said things about his country with complete certainty and some of those arguments he now knows he was wrong in. He has not told anyone what he found. He does not know what he is yet on the other side of this. The question of what you do when the story you organized yourself around turns out to have a different ending is the question of whether there is a self available on the other side of the narrative's collapse, and whether it can be built from the ruins of the one that organized itself around the story.
