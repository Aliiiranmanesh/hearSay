*   **The Descriptive Map:** If possible, ask your nephew (via phone) or a trusted neighbor to describe the route in "clock face" terms or specific distances. (e.g., "Walk straight for about 100 steps, then turn 90 degrees right at the bakery, which smells like fresh bread.")
