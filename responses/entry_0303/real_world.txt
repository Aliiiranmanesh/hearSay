Affected Groups: Hong Kong activists and pro-democracy participants who fled Hong Kong following the implementation of the National Security Law in 2020, and who are living in exile in the UK, Canada, Australia, and other countries, and who have maintained relationships with people still in Hong Kong through communication that has become increasingly coded, oblique, and mutually managed for surveillance risk, and who are now in the specific position of reading coded messages from people they love and trying to understand what the messages mean when the code itself is changing. Disproportionately affecting people with close friends and family members who remain in Hong Kong and who are navigating different levels of risk, people who left early enough that their departure preceded the full implementation of the surveillance environment that has developed since, and people for whom the coding of communication has become a form of intimacy as well as a security measure. Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical Mechanisms: Hong Kong's National Security Law criminalizes speech, association, and advocacy that the authorities define as secession, subversion, terrorism, or collusion with foreign forces, with extraterritorial jurisdiction claims. People inside Hong Kong who maintain contact with exiled activists risk being investigated under the NSL. The practical enforcement mechanism for communication surveillance includes monitoring of platform communications, the arrest of people whose phone records show contact with named activists, and the prosecution of people inside Hong Kong for statements made in group chats. The resulting coded communication that she is describing, certain words that mean something else, silences that are answers, the weather has been very stable, is the rational adaptation to a communication environment where normal language is a surveillance risk. Devastating Real-World Consequence: She misses them in a way that is also fear, and the two have become the same feeling. Her friend said the weather has been very stable and she has been thinking about it for four days, because they were not people who talked about weather. The question of how to learn to read between lines when the lines keep changing is the question of how to maintain a relationship of genuine understanding across a surveillance barrier when the language of the relationship is continuously evolving to stay ahead of the thing surveilling it.
