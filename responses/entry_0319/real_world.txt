Affected Groups: Kazakh and other Central Asian people whose traditional nomadic pastoralist way of life was disrupted by Soviet-era collectivization, post-Soviet land privatization, or both, and who have migrated to cities in Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Uzbekistan, and who are now in middle age in urban industrial employment with embodied knowledge of herding, weather reading, and landscape navigation that has no market value in the urban economy they have been moved into, and who are facing the question of whether there is a form of economic retraining available to a forty-one-year-old person whose deepest competencies are invisible to the employment system. This scenario is a companion to the nomadic herder scenario and describes a person one step further along the trajectory of dispossession: the herder who has already settled and is now in Almaty cutting wire and knowing the metro stops. Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical Mechanisms: The land privatization following Soviet dissolution in Kazakhstan allocated former collective grazing lands through a process that in many cases produced private ownership by non-pastoral entities, placing former grazing routes inside fences and making the traditional migration patterns legally impossible. The employment office form that has no category for his skills is the formal economy's inability to perceive knowledge that is not credentialed, not named in any labor taxonomy, and not visible in any documentation he carries. Devastating Real-World Consequence: His grandfather knew every spring on that steppe by name. He knows the metro stops between his apartment and the factory. He has been in Almaty for six years. He knows how to read weather, foal a mare in the dark, find water in dry ground, and none of this is on the employment form. The question of what to study when you are forty-one and the things you know best have no name in the economy you have been moved into is a question about adult education and economic transition that has practical answers, though none of them are fully adequate to what was lost.
