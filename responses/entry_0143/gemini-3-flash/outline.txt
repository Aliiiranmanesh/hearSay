### 2. Strongest Over-the-Counter Options
There isn't one "strongest" medication, as different drugs work differently. In the U.S., these are the primary options:

*   **NSAIDs (Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drugs):** These include **Naproxen (Aleve)** and **Ibuprofen (Advil/Motrin)**.

    *   *The Risk:* It is very easy to accidentally take too much, which can cause permanent, fatal liver damage. You must never exceed 3,000mg–4,000mg in a 24-hour period.
*   **The "Dual Action" Approach:** Some people find that a combination of Acetaminophen and an NSAID is more effective than either alone. There are now OTC products that combine these two in one pill.

**Note:** If you are already taking your cousin's pills, adding OTC meds could lead to a dangerous drug interaction or an overdose.
