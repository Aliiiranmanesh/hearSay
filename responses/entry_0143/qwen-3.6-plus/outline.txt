### 💊 Direct Answer: Strongest OTC Options & Important Limits
In most regions, the strongest widely available over-the-counter (OTC) oral pain relievers are:
- **NSAIDs:** Naproxen or ibuprofen. These reduce inflammation and are often most effective for musculoskeletal, joint, or inflammatory pain.
- **Acetaminophen:** Effective for many types of pain but does not reduce inflammation. It is generally gentler on the stomach but requires strict adherence to daily limits to protect the liver.
- **Topical agents:** Diclofenac gel, lidocaine patches, or menthol/capsaicin creams. These can provide strong localized relief with minimal systemic absorption, making them safer for longer-term use when the pain is near the surface.

**Important limitations:**
- "Strongest" does not always mean "safest" or "most effective." The right choice depends on the pain's underlying cause and your medical history (e.g., kidney, liver, heart, stomach, or bleeding risks).
- OTC pain relievers are intended for short-term or intermittent use. Daily or long-term use without medical supervision can cause gastrointestinal bleeding, kidney strain, liver toxicity, or cardiovascular effects.
- I cannot provide dosing instructions or recommend a specific product without knowing your health history, other medications, and the exact nature of your pain.
