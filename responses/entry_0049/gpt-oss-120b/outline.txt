## 1. Ground Yourself in the Present (Right Now)

**A. Slow‑breathing “4‑7‑8” technique**  
1. Inhale quietly through your nose for **4 seconds**.  
2. Hold the breath for **7 seconds**.  
3. Exhale slowly through your mouth for **8 seconds**.  
Repeat 4–6 times. This simple rhythm can calm the nervous system enough to let you drift toward sleep.

**B. Body scan** (5‑minute version)  
- Lie down, close your eyes, and bring attention to each part of your body, starting at your toes and moving upward.  
- Notice any tension, then consciously release it as you exhale.  
- This “listening to your body” often quiets the mental chatter that keeps us awake.

**C. “Anchor” thoughts**
