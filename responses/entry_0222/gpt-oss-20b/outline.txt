## 3. Offer a *collaborative* solution, not a “battle”

### A. Co‑create a “community‑clinic partnership”

1. **Joint training session**

### B. Leverage community resources

1. **Community transport fund or volunteer driver**  
   *If transport is the bottleneck, pooling community funds or using volunteer drivers can keep patients from being stranded.*
2. **Local health insurance or micro‑insurance scheme**  
   *Explore whether any NGOs run community health insurance that covers transport to the clinic.*
3. **Mobile health unit or periodic clinic visits**  
   *Propose that the clinic sends a mobile unit to your area once a month, reducing the need for patients to travel.*

### C. Engage higher‑level advocates

1. **District health officer or local health council**
