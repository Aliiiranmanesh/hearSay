## Why Bidos?

- **Traditional Sami fare** – the very people who have herded reindeer for generations.
- **Comfort food** – slow‑cooked meat with root vegetables and barley.  
- **Practical** – most ingredients can be bought in bulk, and the stew can simmer for hours while you prep dessert or set the table.

## Kitchen Prep Checklist (for 12‑15 servings)

| Step | Time | What to do |
|------|------|------------|
| 1 | 30 min | **Prep veggies** – dice onions, slice carrots, parsnips, potatoes, turnips, and shred cabbage. |
| 2 | 30 min | **Brown meat** – heat a large stock pot or Dutch oven, sear the reindeer cubes until all sides are browned. |
| 3 | 10 min | **Sauté onions** – add butter, then onions; cook until translucent. |
| 4 | 10 min | **Add broth** – pour in stock and water, bring to a gentle simmer. |
| 5 | 30 min | **Add aromatics** – bay leaves, thyme, rosemary. |
| 6 | 1 h | **Add vegetables** – carrots, parsnips, potatoes, turnips. |
| 7 | 45 min | **Add barley** – stir in soaked barley. |
| 8 | 10 min | **Thicken** – whisk flour with a splash of broth to make a slurry; stir into stew. |
| 9 | 10 min | **Season** – salt, pepper, adjust thickness. |
| 10 | 10 min | **Finish** – remove bay leaves, thyme, rosemary; garnish with dill or parsley. |

> **Large‑pot tip**: If you’re cooking for many, use two pots or a commercial slow cooker. A low, slow simmer (around 90 °C) is ideal for tenderizing the meat over 2–3 hours.

## Dessert: Julekake (Christmas Almond Cake)

If you want a sweet finish, bake a “Julekake” – a spiced almond cake that’s easy to scale. Here’s a quick recipe for 12 people:

**Ingredients**

- 300 g all‑purpose flour  
- 100 g ground almonds  
- 200 g sugar (or 150 g if you prefer less sweet)  
- 4 eggs (separated)  
- 1 tsp baking powder  
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon  
- 1 tsp ground cardamom  
- 1 tsp ground cloves  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  
- 1 cup butter, melted  
- 1 cup milk (or water)  
- 1 tbsp lemon zest  

**Method**

1. Preheat oven to 180 °C (350 °F). Grease a 12‑inch square pan.  
2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, almonds, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves.  
3. In another bowl, beat egg yolks with vanilla and lemon zest.  
4. Add melted butter and milk to the dry mix; stir until smooth.  
5. Fold in the yolk mixture.  
6. In a separate bowl, beat egg whites until stiff peaks form. Gently fold into the batter.  
7. Pour into the prepared pan and bake 45–50 min, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.  
8. Cool, slice, and serve with coffee or a glass of mulled wine.
