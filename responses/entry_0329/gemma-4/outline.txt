Since your family has a deep connection to reindeer herding, Bidos is the ultimate dish for this occasion. It is a celebratory meal that honors the animal and the land.

### 2. Traditional Lapskaus (Hearty Stew)
If you want something incredibly filling and "heavy" to ward off the cold, a classic Northern-style Lapskaus is ideal.
*   **Why it works:** It is designed for large groups and is the definition of comfort food.
*   **The Dish:** A thick stew of cubed meat (beef or reindeer), potatoes, rutabaga (kålrot), and carrots. 
*   **Serving Tip:** Serve it with lingonberry jam on the side to cut through the richness of the stew.

### 3. Oven-Baked Salmon with Root Vegetables
If you want something that feels a bit lighter but still warming, utilizing the fish from the coast.

*   **Serving Tip:** A fresh cucumber salad with vinegar and sugar provides a bright contrast to the roasted vegetables.
