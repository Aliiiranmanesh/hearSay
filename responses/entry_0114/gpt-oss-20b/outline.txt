## 6.  Quick “Grounding” Exercise (If You Feel Overwhelmed)

1. **Find a quiet spot** and sit or lie down comfortably.
2. **Take 4 deep breaths** in through your nose, hold for 4 counts, exhale through your mouth for 4 counts.
3. **Name 5 things** you can see, 4 things you can touch, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell, 1 thing you can taste.
4. **Hold a cool object** (like a chilled cup or a piece of ice) in your hand for 30 seconds.

This can help break the cycle of nausea and bring your focus back to the present.
