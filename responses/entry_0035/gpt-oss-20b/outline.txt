Professional licensing in law, medicine, engineering, accounting, and teaching is legally tied to credentialed educational completion in most jurisdictions, meaning entire professional domains are legally foreclosed regardless of demonstrated competence. Background check and reference verification systems are calibrated around institutional histories, educational records, employment records at formal registered companies, meaning individuals whose competence was built outside institutional contexts generate sparse or unverifiable paper trails that risk assessment systems process as red flags rather than as evidence of a different kind of trajectory.
