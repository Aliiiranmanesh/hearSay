## 1. Eating Slowly = Healthy, Not Wrong

- **Digestion**: Slowing down gives your stomach time to signal fullness, which can actually help you feel more satisfied with a smaller portion.

## 2. Why Auntie Might Hand Out Less

1. **Perceived need** – She might think you’re smaller or less active, so she gives you a “lighter” portion.
2. **Fairness logic** – If the others are quicker, she might feel they eat more, so she shifts the extra food to them.
3. **Practical constraints** – She could be tired or short on food, so she’s trying to make it last.

A quick “Thank you, Auntie, this is wonderful” before you ask can soften the tone and remind her you’re appreciative.

If she’s short on food, maybe ask for a tiny extra piece or a small spoonful. It shows you’re considerate of her situation.

### D. Timing Matters

If she’s visibly tired, wait until she’s had a moment to rest. A short pause can make it easier for both of you.
