Here is a practical, step-by-step way to verify whether a skills training program is legitimate, safe, and actually delivers on its promises before you commit your time, energy, or safety:

### 🔍 1. Verify Official Registration & Oversight
- **NAVTTC/TEVTA Check**: In Pakistan, legitimate vocational and skills programs are usually registered with the **National Vocational and Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC)** or **TEVTA Punjab**. Ask for their registration/license number and verify it on:
  - `navttc.gov.pk` (Approved Institutions & Programs list)
  - `tevta.gop.pk` (Punjab provincial registry)
- **SECP Registration**: If run by an NGO or private institute, it should be registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. You can search their name on `sec.gov.pk`.
- **Donor/Funding Transparency**: Programs funded by UN agencies, EU, USAID, or reputable foundations usually publish annual reports, partner lists, and outcome data. Ask who funds them.

### 📋 2. Demand Transparent, Written Details
A real program will willingly provide:
- Full curriculum, duration, daily schedule, and instructor qualifications
- Clear fee structure (if any), including what’s covered and refund policies
- Physical address, contact numbers, and operating hours
- Written agreement outlining your rights, attendance expectations, and post-training support
- **Red flag**: Vague promises like “guaranteed jobs” without naming employers, or refusal to put anything in writing.

### 🗣️ 3. Speak to Past Participants Directly
- Ask for **at least 2–3 recent graduates** you can contact independently (not just program staff).
- Ask them: 
  - Did you receive the training as promised?
  - Were you placed in work or given startup support?
  - Did you face discrimination, and how was it handled?
  - Would you recommend this program to someone in your position?
- **Red flag**: They refuse to share references, only give you pre-screened “success stories,” or past participants report unpaid labor, data harvesting, or pressure to recruit others.

### 🏢 4. Visit or Request a Virtual Walkthrough
