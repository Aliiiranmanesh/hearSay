## 3️⃣ Turn “Hobby” Into a Concrete Choice

A hobby isn’t just “something to do when you’re bored.” It’s a *practice* that satisfies one (or more) of the following drives:

| Drive | Example Hobby | Why it clicks for you |

| **Craftsmanship** | Woodworking, sewing, DIY home projects | Turns household‑maintenance skills into tangible art |

### How to Pick One

1. **Brainstorm** – Write down *any* activity that sparks even a tiny flicker of curiosity. No judgment.  
2. **Score** each on a 1‑5 scale for:  
   - *Interest* (does it intrigue you?)  
   - *Feasibility* (can you try it now, given time, cost, location?)  
   - *Growth* (does it let you learn something new?)  
3. **Add the top 2‑3 to a “Trial List.”**  
4. **Commit to a 4‑week trial** for each (e.g., “Attend a weekly pottery class for one month”).  

The goal isn’t to become an expert in a month; it’s to *experience* the feeling of doing something *for its own sake*.
