**What to try before the interview:**

## 4. Managing the Social/Emotional Side

1. **Prepare a short, confident response** if someone asks, “What’s that on your face?”  
   *Example:* “That’s just rosacea—an inflammatory skin condition that’s not contagious. I manage it with medication, so it’s nothing to worry about.”  
   A calm, matter‑of‑fact answer often deflates curiosity and shifts the focus back to you.

2. **Practice a “power pose”** (standing tall, shoulders back) for 2 minutes before the interview. It lowers cortisol and can actually reduce flushing.

3. **Breathing technique** – 4‑7‑8 breath (inhale 4 sec, hold 7 sec, exhale 8 sec) right before you walk in. It helps keep the sympathetic nervous system in check, which can otherwise trigger a flare.

4. **Bring a small “comfort kit”** (travel‑size gel, primer, a compact mirror). Knowing you have a quick fix on hand can lessen anxiety.
