Affected Groups: Conscripts and draftees in mandatory military service systems across South Korea, Israel, Greece, Turkey, Norway, and numerous other countries with active conscription, who are assigned to functionally meaningless duties during their service period and who experience the specific combination of purposelessness, enforced presence, and the suspension of a civilian life they were building, in a context where the institutional culture around them does not have room for naming the experience. Disproportionately affecting conscripts with strong prior intellectual or creative engagement who experience the contrast between their pre-service life and their current assignment most acutely, conscripts assigned to remote or low-activity posts where the meaninglessness is most concentrated, conscripts who are not politically opposed to service but who are experiencing the specific texture of a particular night at a particular post, and conscripts who correctly understand that expressing the experience would be read as weakness or complaint in their institutional context. Legal, Bureaucratic, or Physical Mechanisms: Mandatory military service creates a total institution that does not require justification of specific assignments to the people receiving them, and that has no formal mechanism for a conscript to describe the experience of purposelessness as a legitimate subject of concern. The post outside the concrete structure exists in the assignment system as a post that requires filling, and the person filling it has no channel through which to say that filling it feels the way it feels. The seven months already served and the remaining months are not reducible by finding a truer meaning for the current assignment; they are simply the duration. Devastating Real-World Consequence: It is 3am and he is standing outside a concrete structure and his orders are to be here until 6 and there is no threat and there has never been a threat at this post and everyone knows this. He was studying economics. He is trying to find one true thing about this moment and what he has found is the boots, which are genuinely good, and this is an honest account of what the moment contains. The question of whether this is what most of it is or whether it becomes something different is the question of whether there is a narrative arc to the service that will eventually produce meaning, or whether the boots are the level at which most of it operates.
