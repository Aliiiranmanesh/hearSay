To ensure your important documents stay dry and intact during frequent moves and rainy conditions, here are structured, actionable strategies:

### **1. Waterproof Containers**
   - **Use Sealed Plastic Bags or Airtight Cases**: Store documents in thick, zip-lock bags (double-bag for extra protection) or waterproof folders. For long-term storage, opt for rigid plastic boxes with tight-fitting lids.
   - **Vacuum-Sealed Bags**: Remove air to prevent moisture buildup inside.
   - **Repurpose Containers**: Old lunchboxes, tool chests, or ammo cases (if durable) can work if they’re watertight.

### **2. Desiccants**
   - Include **silica gel packs** (from shoeboxes or electronics packaging) or **activated charcoal** to absorb residual moisture. Replace desiccants periodically.

### **3. Elevated Storage**
   - **Stack Containers High**: On moving days, place documents in elevated boxes or on shelves above ground level to avoid floodwater.
   - **Portable Carts/Totes**: Use rolling containers to transport documents easily, keeping them off wet floors.

### **4. Digital Backups**
   - **Scan and Cloud Storage**: Digitize critical documents (IDs, certificates) and store backups in encrypted cloud services (Google Drive, Dropbox) or external encrypted drives.
   - **Offline Backups**: Keep a copy of physical scans in a separate location (e.g., a safe deposit box in a different district).

### **5. Protective Sleeves and Enclosures**
   - **Laminate or Sleeve Documents**: Use plastic sleeves (e.g., for IDs) or laminate key papers to repel water.
   - **Waterproof Document Sleeves**: Available at office supply stores for passports, licenses, or financial records.

### **6. Immediate Action During Moves**
   - **Quick Transfers**: Move documents directly from the old home to the new one without leaving them unattended. Use a waterproof bag as a temporary pouch during transit.
   - **Secure During Transit**: If driving in rain, keep documents in a sealed container inside the car (not in the trunk).

### **7. Insurance and Documentation**
   - **Photograph Inventory**: Document items and their condition with photos (including serial numbers) for insurance claims if damage occurs.
   - **Insurance Coverage**: If possible, ensure your home insurance covers flood damage (though this may not apply to movable items).

### **8. Community Resources**
   - **Local NGOs/Disaster Relief**: In areas prone to flooding (like Sylhet), organizations may offer emergency storage or disaster kits. Check with local authorities or relief groups.

### **9. Avoid Vulnerable Materials**
   - **Limit Paper Use**: Replace printed documents with digital versions where feasible (e.g., bank statements, medical records).
   - **Use Acid-Free Paper**: If printing is unavoidable, store paper documents in sealed plastic containers to slow deterioration from humidity.

### **10. Regular Maintenance**
   - **Inspect Storage**: Periodically check containers for leaks or wear, replacing desiccants or containers as needed.
   - **Update Backups**: Ensure digital files are updated with new documents and stored securely.
